<h2 id="how-to-sell-canva-templates-online-without-breaking-the-rules">How to sell Canva templates online without breaking the rules</h2>
<p>Canva lets you sell designs you make with its tools, but the rules about its content library are strict, and breaking them can get your shop closed or your account banned. The most important rule is that you must create the template yourself. You cannot take an existing Canva template, change one colour, and sell it as your own work.</p>
<p>Canva explains this in its own licensing article, <a href="https://www.canva.com/learn/content-licensing-agreement-explained-stock-images-copyright/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Content licensing: What you need to know</a>, which was updated on 2026-05-26. The article says that existing templates on Canva cannot be resold as they are, and the safest way to sell a template is to build it from scratch with your own text, fonts, colours and layout.</p>
<p>The second rule concerns Pro Content, which means the photos, graphics, fonts and other elements marked as Pro in Canva. If your template includes Pro Content, you can only sell it as a shareable link that sends the buyer back to Canva. You cannot sell it as a file to download, such as a PDF, and you cannot sell the content on its own. Your listing must explain clearly that buyers will receive a link, not a download.</p>
<p>Free users who open a template with Pro Content will see a watermark, and they can buy a one-off licence for a single design or upgrade to a Pro plan. This is a common cause of complaints, so design with free fonts and elements where you can, or warn buyers clearly that they need Canva Pro for the full design. Read the full Content License Agreement before you list anything, because Canva changes its terms from time to time, and the agreement is the document that governs your use.</p>
<h2 id="canva-creators-or-selling-on-your-own">Canva Creators or selling on your own</h2>
<p>Canva runs its own template marketplace through the Canva Creators programme. Creators submit templates to Canva, and Canva pays them royalties when people use the templates. You do not set the price, and you do not own the customer relationship, but you also do not handle listings, payments or support.</p>
<p>The trade off is control. Templates accepted into the Creators programme are exclusive to Canva for three years, according to competitor research checked in November 2026, so you cannot sell the same template on Etsy during that period. Confirm the current exclusivity period in the Creators terms before you apply. Selling on your own gives you control of pricing, branding and the customer list, but listings, delivery, refunds and marketing are all yours.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-design-a-template-pack-buyers-can-actually-use">How to design a template pack buyers can actually use</h2>
<p>A good template pack is easy to edit. Buyers are not designers, so every part of the template must be obvious. Keep text in real text boxes rather than baked into images, and leave space for a buyer to add their own logo or photo.</p>
<p>Start with free fonts and free elements where possible. If a template uses a Pro font, a buyer on a free Canva account will see a watermark or be asked to pay, and that leads to refund requests. If you do use Pro elements, say so in the listing in plain words, for example "This template uses Canva Pro fonts, so you need a Canva Pro account or a one-off licence to use it as shown."</p>
<p>Add a short instructions PDF with every pack that explains how to open the link, make a copy, change colours and fonts, and download the finished design. Keep it to one or two pages. Test the template on a phone as well as on a computer, because text that looks fine on a laptop can overlap on a small screen. Check every page, not just the first one.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-create-and-test-a-template-link">How to create and test a template link</h2>
<p>A template link is the product. When a buyer clicks it, Canva should open a copy of your template in their account, ready to edit. To make one, open your design in Canva, choose Share, then Template link, and copy the link. Never share the edit link for your original design, because that would let a buyer change your master copy.</p>
<p>Test the link before you list it. Open it in a private browser window while signed out of Canva, or ask a friend to open it from their own account. Check that it opens a fresh copy and that the fonts and colours look right. Test it again from a free Canva account if you can, because that is how many of your buyers will open it. If you edit the master design later, make a new link and update your delivery file.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-set-up-an-etsy-listing-step-by-step">How to set up an Etsy listing step by step</h2>
<p>A good Etsy listing answers every question a buyer might have before they pay. Start with a clear title that names the product, the use and the format, for example "20 Instagram Post Templates for Fitness Coaches, Canva Template Link". Use all 13 tags Etsy allows, with phrases buyers search for, such as "canva template", "instagram templates" and "fitness coach templates".</p>
<p>Your mockups matter more than your words. Show the templates in use, on a phone screen and on a laptop, and show several different pages from the pack. Buyers want to see the range they are paying for. If you can, add a short video that shows you editing one template in Canva, because it proves the templates work.</p>
<p>The description should state the delivery method in the first lines: "You will receive a PDF with a Canva template link. This is not a file download. Click the link to open your own copy in Canva." Then list what is included, the number of templates, the sizes, the fonts used, and whether Canva Pro is needed.</p>
<p>Delivery works through a PDF. After a buyer pays, Etsy lets them download the PDF, which contains the template link and the instructions. Test the download yourself after you publish the listing. If a buyer cannot open the link, reply fast with a fresh link and clear steps.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-market-your-templates">How to market your templates</h2>
<p>Marketing starts with showing the templates in use. Post before and after examples on Pinterest and Instagram, where your buyers already look for design ideas. A Pinterest pin that shows a finished Instagram post gets more clicks than a pin that just shows a product photo. For a full channel plan, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/digital-products/how-to-sell-digital-products-with-pinterest" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to sell digital products with Pinterest</a>.</p>
<p>Give away a small free pack, such as three templates, in exchange for an email address. That list becomes your best sales channel, because you can tell past buyers when you launch a new pack. For pricing help, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/digital-products/how-to-price-digital-products" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to price digital products</a>, and for the first steps of building a shop, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/digital-products/how-to-create-your-first-digital-product" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to create your first digital product</a>.</p>
<p>Do not offer free products in exchange for reviews, because that breaks Etsy rules.</p>
<h2 id="what-it-costs-and-what-to-expect">What it costs and what to expect</h2>
<p>You can start with very little money. You can start on the free Canva plan with free fonts and elements, and upgrade to Pro only if you use Pro elements often. Etsy charges $0.20 per listing, and you may pay a one-off shop set up fee depending on your country, so check <a href="https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Etsy's Fees and Payments Policy</a> before you open a shop.</p>
<p>Expect a slow start. Most new shops take weeks or months to get regular sales, because they have no reviews and little search history. Plan to list at least 10 to 15 packs before you judge the shop. Do not expect steady daily income in the first months, and do not spend money on ads until you know which listings already sell.</p>
<h2 id="taxes-refunds-and-terms-of-use">Taxes, refunds and terms of use</h2>
<p>Digital goods are taxed differently in different countries, and marketplaces handle some of the tax for you. Etsy collects and remits VAT and sales tax on many orders, but you still need to report your own income to your local tax authority. Keep a simple record of every sale, fee and refund, and speak to a qualified accountant if your sales grow.</p>
<p>Write a short terms of use page for your shop. It should say that the buyer gets a licence to use the templates for their own business, that they may not resell or share the templates, and that refunds are not given once the link has been used, unless the link does not work. If a buyer cannot open the link, fix it or refund them, because a refund costs less than a bad review.</p>
<h2 id="common-mistakes">Common mistakes</h2>
<ul>
<li>Selling a Canva template you did not build yourself, which breaks Canva's licence rules and risks your shop.</li>
<li>Using Pro fonts and graphics without telling buyers.</li>
<li>Sharing the edit link to your master design instead of a template link, which lets buyers change your original.</li>
<li>Writing a listing that never says the buyer receives a link, not a file, which causes confusion after payment.</li>
<li>Skipping the test step, so the first buyer finds a broken link and leaves a one star review.</li>
<li>Copying another seller's mockups, titles or descriptions, which can lead to a copyright complaint.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="risks-and-scams-to-watch-for">Risks and scams to watch for</h2>
<p>Be careful of buyers who ask you to send the template link before they pay, or who ask you to move the sale off Etsy to avoid fees. Both are against marketplace rules, and you lose all protection if you agree. Watch for messages that claim your listing breaks a rule and ask you to click a link to fix it, because these are often phishing messages. Only follow links from the official marketplace site or app. Do not buy reviews, followers or fake sales, because marketplaces detect this quickly and the penalty is usually a closed shop.</p>
<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 id="is-it-legal-to-sell-canva-templates">Is it legal to sell Canva templates?</h3>
<p>Yes, it is legal to sell templates you design yourself in Canva, as long as you follow Canva's Content License Agreement. You must build the template from scratch, and if it includes Pro Content, you can only sell it as a shareable link back to Canva.</p>
<h3 id="do-i-need-canva-pro-to-sell-templates">Do I need Canva Pro to sell templates?</h3>
<p>No, you do not need Canva Pro to sell templates, and many sellers start on the free plan. Templates made with free elements are easier for buyers on free accounts to use, so start free and upgrade only when Pro features would clearly improve your packs.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy">Can I sell Canva templates on Etsy?</h3>
<p>Yes, Etsy is one of the most common places to sell Canva templates. You deliver the product as a PDF that contains the template link and instructions. Your listing must state clearly that buyers receive a link, not a file, and whether Canva Pro is needed.</p>
<h3 id="how-do-i-deliver-canva-templates-to-buyers">How do I deliver Canva templates to buyers?</h3>
<p>You deliver a PDF that contains the template link and short instructions. On Etsy, the buyer downloads the PDF after payment and clicks the link to open their own copy in Canva. Always test the link from a fresh account before you list it.</p>
<h3 id="how-much-can-you-earn-selling-canva-templates">How much can you earn selling Canva templates?</h3>
<p>There is no reliable published average, and earnings vary widely by niche, price, number of listings and marketing. Treat it as a slow business that grows with each new pack and each good review, not as quick income.</p>
<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2>
<p>To sell Canva templates online, build original templates from scratch, follow Canva's licence rules for Pro Content, test every template link before you list it, and price your packs against similar listings. Start with one niche, list at least ten packs, and improve the listings that get views.</p>
