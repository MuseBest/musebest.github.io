How to Sell Canva Templates Online in 2026: A Guide

To sell Canva templates online, you design original templates in Canva and sell buyers a shareable link that opens a copy in their own Canva account. You do not send a file, because a Canva template only works inside Canva. This guide explains the licence rules, the places to sell, how to price a pack, and how to set up an Etsy listing that buyers can actually use.

How to sell Canva templates online without breaking the rules

Canva lets you sell designs you make with its tools, but the rules about its content library are strict, and breaking them can get your shop closed or your account banned. The most important rule is that you must create the template yourself. You cannot take an existing Canva template, change one colour, and sell it as your own work.

Canva explains this in its own licensing article, Content licensing: What you need to know, which was updated on 2026-05-26. The article says that existing templates on Canva cannot be resold as they are, and the safest way to sell a template is to build it from scratch with your own text, fonts, colours and layout.

The second rule concerns Pro Content, which means the photos, graphics, fonts and other elements marked as Pro in Canva. If your template includes Pro Content, you can only sell it as a shareable link that sends the buyer back to Canva. You cannot sell it as a file to download, such as a PDF, and you cannot sell the content on its own. Your listing must explain clearly that buyers will receive a link, not a download.

Free users who open a template with Pro Content will see a watermark, and they can buy a one-off licence for a single design or upgrade to a Pro plan. This is a common cause of complaints, so design with free fonts and elements where you can, or warn buyers clearly that they need Canva Pro for the full design. Read the full Content License Agreement before you list anything, because Canva changes its terms from time to time, and the agreement is the document that governs your use.

Canva Creators or selling on your own

Canva runs its own template marketplace through the Canva Creators programme. Creators submit templates to Canva, and Canva pays them royalties when people use the templates. You do not set the price, and you do not own the customer relationship, but you also do not handle listings, payments or support.

The trade off is control. Templates accepted into the Creators programme are exclusive to Canva for three years, according to competitor research checked in November 2026, so you cannot sell the same template on Etsy during that period. Confirm the current exclusivity period in the Creators terms before you apply. Selling on your own gives you control of pricing, branding and the customer list, but listings, delivery, refunds and marketing are all yours.

What kinds of Canva templates sell best

Templates sell when they save a busy person time. The strongest niches are the ones where buyers post or present every week and need a consistent look.

Template type Who buys it Why it sells
Instagram and Pinterest posts Coaches, small shops, creators They need fresh posts every week
Media kits and price lists Freelancers and small businesses They need to look professional fast
Resumes and cover letters Job seekers They want a clean layout without design skills
Planners and trackers Students and busy parents They like printable or digital planning

Pick one niche before you design anything. A shop with 20 templates for fitness coaches will outsell a shop with 20 unrelated templates, because buyers trust a seller who understands their work.

How to design a template pack buyers can actually use

A good template pack is easy to edit. Buyers are not designers, so every part of the template must be obvious. Keep text in real text boxes rather than baked into images, and leave space for a buyer to add their own logo or photo.

Start with free fonts and free elements where possible. If a template uses a Pro font, a buyer on a free Canva account will see a watermark or be asked to pay, and that leads to refund requests. If you do use Pro elements, say so in the listing in plain words, for example "This template uses Canva Pro fonts, so you need a Canva Pro account or a one-off licence to use it as shown."

Add a short instructions PDF with every pack that explains how to open the link, make a copy, change colours and fonts, and download the finished design. Keep it to one or two pages. Test the template on a phone as well as on a computer, because text that looks fine on a laptop can overlap on a small screen. Check every page, not just the first one.

How to create and test a template link

A template link is the product. When a buyer clicks it, Canva should open a copy of your template in their account, ready to edit. To make one, open your design in Canva, choose Share, then Template link, and copy the link. Never share the edit link for your original design, because that would let a buyer change your master copy.

Test the link before you list it. Open it in a private browser window while signed out of Canva, or ask a friend to open it from their own account. Check that it opens a fresh copy and that the fonts and colours look right. Test it again from a free Canva account if you can, because that is how many of your buyers will open it. If you edit the master design later, make a new link and update your delivery file.

How to price your Canva templates

Pricing depends on how many templates are in the pack and how much work each one saves. A single simple template usually sells for a few dollars, while a large pack of matching templates can sell for much more. Bundles work well because buyers feel they get a better deal.

Pack size Typical price range Best for
Single template $3 to $10 Testing a new niche
Small pack, 5 to 10 templates $8 to $20 First product in a shop
Large pack, 20 to 40 templates $15 to $35 Social media kits
Bundle of several packs $47 to $97 Best value offers and sales

These ranges come from competitor guides checked in November 2026, and they are a starting point, not a rule. Check the prices of similar packs in your niche before you set yours. A price that is far above the rest of the market needs a clear reason, such as more templates, a video tutorial, or a commercial licence.

Example only: imagine you sell a pack of 20 Instagram templates for fitness coaches at $15 on Etsy. Etsy's Fees and Payments Policy, checked on etsy.com in November 2026, charges a $0.20 listing fee and a 6.5 percent transaction fee. The transaction fee on $15 is $0.98, because 6.5 percent of $15 is $0.975, rounded to $0.98. Add the $0.20 listing fee and the Etsy fees come to $1.18 per sale, before payment processing fees, which vary by country. If 40 packs sell in a month, gross sales are 40 times $15, which is $600, and Etsy listing and transaction fees are 40 times $1.18, which is $47.20, leaving $552.80 before payment processing fees and tax. These numbers are an example only, and they are not typical results.

Where to sell Canva templates: Etsy, Creative Market and your own store

Etsy is the most common first choice, because buyers already search there for templates. Creative Market suits sellers with a strong design portfolio. Your own store gives you the most control and the lowest fees per sale, but you must bring your own traffic.

Channel Fees as of November 2026 Strengths Drawbacks
Etsy $0.20 listing fee, 6.5 percent transaction fee, plus payment processing fees that vary by country Huge buyer traffic, trusted checkout Fees on every sale, lots of competition
Creative Market Commission reported at 50 percent by secondary sources, not verified on its site Design focused buyers, clean shop pages High commission, strict quality review
Your own store Payment processor fees only, plus any platform fee Full control, keep the customer list No built in traffic, you do the marketing

The Creative Market commission figure is reported by other guides and was not verified on Creative Market's own site in this session, so check its seller terms before you rely on it. For a $15 sale on Etsy, the listing and transaction fees are about $1.18, as shown in the example above. On your own store, you keep far more of the $15, but you must pay for the store and find the buyers yourself. Many sellers list on Etsy first, then add their own store once they have an email list.

How to set up an Etsy listing step by step

A good Etsy listing answers every question a buyer might have before they pay. Start with a clear title that names the product, the use and the format, for example "20 Instagram Post Templates for Fitness Coaches, Canva Template Link". Use all 13 tags Etsy allows, with phrases buyers search for, such as "canva template", "instagram templates" and "fitness coach templates".

Your mockups matter more than your words. Show the templates in use, on a phone screen and on a laptop, and show several different pages from the pack. Buyers want to see the range they are paying for. If you can, add a short video that shows you editing one template in Canva, because it proves the templates work.

The description should state the delivery method in the first lines: "You will receive a PDF with a Canva template link. This is not a file download. Click the link to open your own copy in Canva." Then list what is included, the number of templates, the sizes, the fonts used, and whether Canva Pro is needed.

Delivery works through a PDF. After a buyer pays, Etsy lets them download the PDF, which contains the template link and the instructions. Test the download yourself after you publish the listing. If a buyer cannot open the link, reply fast with a fresh link and clear steps.

How to market your templates

Marketing starts with showing the templates in use. Post before and after examples on Pinterest and Instagram, where your buyers already look for design ideas. A Pinterest pin that shows a finished Instagram post gets more clicks than a pin that just shows a product photo. For a full channel plan, read how to sell digital products with Pinterest.

Give away a small free pack, such as three templates, in exchange for an email address. That list becomes your best sales channel, because you can tell past buyers when you launch a new pack. For pricing help, read how to price digital products, and for the first steps of building a shop, read how to create your first digital product.

Do not offer free products in exchange for reviews, because that breaks Etsy rules.

What it costs and what to expect

You can start with very little money. You can start on the free Canva plan with free fonts and elements, and upgrade to Pro only if you use Pro elements often. Etsy charges $0.20 per listing, and you may pay a one-off shop set up fee depending on your country, so check Etsy's Fees and Payments Policy before you open a shop.

Expect a slow start. Most new shops take weeks or months to get regular sales, because they have no reviews and little search history. Plan to list at least 10 to 15 packs before you judge the shop. Do not expect steady daily income in the first months, and do not spend money on ads until you know which listings already sell.

Taxes, refunds and terms of use

Digital goods are taxed differently in different countries, and marketplaces handle some of the tax for you. Etsy collects and remits VAT and sales tax on many orders, but you still need to report your own income to your local tax authority. Keep a simple record of every sale, fee and refund, and speak to a qualified accountant if your sales grow.

Write a short terms of use page for your shop. It should say that the buyer gets a licence to use the templates for their own business, that they may not resell or share the templates, and that refunds are not given once the link has been used, unless the link does not work. If a buyer cannot open the link, fix it or refund them, because a refund costs less than a bad review.

Common mistakes

  • Selling a Canva template you did not build yourself, which breaks Canva's licence rules and risks your shop.
  • Using Pro fonts and graphics without telling buyers.
  • Sharing the edit link to your master design instead of a template link, which lets buyers change your original.
  • Writing a listing that never says the buyer receives a link, not a file, which causes confusion after payment.
  • Skipping the test step, so the first buyer finds a broken link and leaves a one star review.
  • Copying another seller's mockups, titles or descriptions, which can lead to a copyright complaint.

Risks and scams to watch for

Be careful of buyers who ask you to send the template link before they pay, or who ask you to move the sale off Etsy to avoid fees. Both are against marketplace rules, and you lose all protection if you agree. Watch for messages that claim your listing breaks a rule and ask you to click a link to fix it, because these are often phishing messages. Only follow links from the official marketplace site or app. Do not buy reviews, followers or fake sales, because marketplaces detect this quickly and the penalty is usually a closed shop.

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to sell Canva templates?

Yes, it is legal to sell templates you design yourself in Canva, as long as you follow Canva's Content License Agreement. You must build the template from scratch, and if it includes Pro Content, you can only sell it as a shareable link back to Canva.

Do I need Canva Pro to sell templates?

No, you do not need Canva Pro to sell templates, and many sellers start on the free plan. Templates made with free elements are easier for buyers on free accounts to use, so start free and upgrade only when Pro features would clearly improve your packs.

Can I sell Canva templates on Etsy?

Yes, Etsy is one of the most common places to sell Canva templates. You deliver the product as a PDF that contains the template link and instructions. Your listing must state clearly that buyers receive a link, not a file, and whether Canva Pro is needed.

How do I deliver Canva templates to buyers?

You deliver a PDF that contains the template link and short instructions. On Etsy, the buyer downloads the PDF after payment and clicks the link to open their own copy in Canva. Always test the link from a fresh account before you list it.

How much can you earn selling Canva templates?

There is no reliable published average, and earnings vary widely by niche, price, number of listings and marketing. Treat it as a slow business that grows with each new pack and each good review, not as quick income.

Conclusion

To sell Canva templates online, build original templates from scratch, follow Canva's licence rules for Pro Content, test every template link before you list it, and price your packs against similar listings. Start with one niche, list at least ten packs, and improve the listings that get views.
