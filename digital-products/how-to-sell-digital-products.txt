1f:T9b7,<h2 id="what-counts-as-a-digital-product">What counts as a digital product</h2>
<p>Anything a customer can download or access online: a spreadsheet template, a design template, a preset pack, an ebook, a short course, a set of icons, a Notion system. The product is made once and sold many times, which is why the margins are attractive — and why the competition is real.</p>
<h2 id="start-with-a-problem-not-a-product">Start with a problem, not a product</h2>
<p>The products that sell answer a question a buyer is already asking: "How do I invoice clients?", "How do I plan a wedding on a budget?", "How do I edit photos faster?" Ask in communities, read reviews of competing products, and note what buyers complain is missing.</p>
<h2 id="validate-before-you-build">Validate before you build</h2>
<p>Before spending weeks on a product, test the idea:</p>
<ol>
<li>Describe it in one paragraph and share it where your audience is.</li>
<li>Offer a simple version or a pre-order to ten people.</li>
<li>If nobody is interested, change the idea — not the marketing.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="where-to-sell">Where to sell</h2>
<p>Marketplaces bring built-in traffic and take a fee; your own website keeps more of the price but needs you to bring the buyers. Many sellers start on a marketplace to learn what sells, then move their best products to their own store.</p>
<h2 id="pricing">Pricing</h2>
<p>Price by the value to the buyer, not by your hours. A template that saves a freelancer an hour a week is worth more than a template that saves ten minutes once. Look at comparable products, then price in the same range until you have reviews — and raise the price when the product improves.</p>
<h2 id="what-buyers-expect">What buyers expect</h2>
<ul>
<li>A clear description of exactly what is included, with screenshots or a preview.</li>
<li>Files that work as described, with instructions.</li>
<li>A way to contact you if something does not work.</li>
<li>A refund policy stated upfront. Digital products have higher refund expectations than many sellers assume, so be clear about what you offer.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-honest-truth-about-quot-passive-quot-income">The honest truth about "passive" income</h2>
<p>Digital products are not passive at the start. You will spend far more time making, listing, and promoting the product than you will spend on each sale. The income becomes steadier only after you have an audience, good reviews, and a product that people recommend.</p>
