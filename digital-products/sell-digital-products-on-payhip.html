How to Sell Digital Products on Payhip in 2026

To sell digital products on Payhip, you create a free account, upload your file, connect Stripe or PayPal, set a price, and share your store link. Payhip then handles the checkout, the file delivery, and the tax paperwork for digital sales in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. This guide walks you through each step, explains the real fees, and shows you what to expect in your first months.

What Payhip is and who it suits

Payhip is an online platform built for creators who sell digital products directly to their audience. It is based in the United Kingdom and has served creators for more than ten years. You can sell ebooks, templates, presets, software, online courses, memberships, coaching sessions, and even physical products from the same account.

The platform suits you if you already have an audience, even a small one, and you want a simple store without building a website or paying a monthly fee. It suits you less if you want deep control over how your store looks, or if you need advanced search engine tools. Payhip keeps its store builder simple on purpose, so the trade is ease against control.

Every plan includes the same features. The difference between the plans is only the fee you pay on each sale. That is unusual, and it is the main reason Payhip appeals to beginners. You can read the full list of features and fees on Payhip's official features page.

Payhip plan Monthly cost as of November 2026 Fee per sale Best for
Free 0 US dollars 5 percent Testing your first product
Plus 29 US dollars 2 percent Regular sales over 1,000 US dollars a month
Pro 99 US dollars 0 percent High volume sellers

On top of the Payhip fee, your payment processor takes its own cut. Stripe charges 2.9 percent plus 0.30 US dollars on a standard domestic card payment in the United States, and rates differ by country, so check the rate for your own country before you set prices. PayPal fees also vary by country and payment method.

Step 1: Pick one product to sell first

Start with one product that solves one clear problem. A 20 page guide that fixes a single problem sells better than a 200 page book that tries to cover everything. Ask yourself what people already ask you for help with, and what shortcut you can package into a file.

Good first products on Payhip include a Notion template, a set of Canva templates, a Lightroom preset pack, a short ebook, a spreadsheet, or a printable planner. Each of these is a single file, or a small zip file, which keeps the setup simple.

Before you build anything, check that people want it. Post about the problem on social media or in a community you belong to, and watch the replies. If nobody reacts, you have saved yourself a weekend of building. If you need help shaping the idea, read our guide on how to create your first digital product.

Step 2: Create your Payhip account and store

Go to Payhip and sign up with your name and email address. No card details are needed for the free plan. The sign up takes about two minutes.

After you register, Payhip asks you to name your store. This name becomes part of your store address, in the form payhip.com/yourstorename, so choose it with care. You can connect your own domain later at no extra charge from Payhip, though you still pay a domain registrar for the domain itself.

Once your store exists, Payhip shows you a short setup list: add your first product, set up your payment options, and customise the layout of your store. Work through it in that order. Do not spend hours on colours and fonts before you have a product to sell.

Step 3: Connect payments so you can get paid

Open your Payhip dashboard and connect at least one payment gateway. Stripe and PayPal cover most sellers. Connect both if you can, because some buyers trust PayPal more than entering card details on a new store, and others prefer to pay by card.

Payhip now supports 13 payment gateways, including Mollie for Europe, Razorpay for India, Paystack and Flutterwave for several African countries, Mercado Pago for Latin America, Square, PayU, Iyzico, Midtrans, Xendit, and PayTabs. Which ones you can use depends on where your business is registered.

Money lands in your Stripe or PayPal account as soon as the payment clears. Payhip does not hold your money or pay you in weekly batches. That matters when you want to reinvest early sales into ads or a new product.

Step 4: Add your first product and set the price

Click Add Product and choose your product type. Payhip supports five types: digital downloads, online courses, memberships, coaching, and physical products. For a first sale, choose digital downloads.

Upload your file, give the product a clear name, and write a short description that says what is inside, who it is for, and what result the buyer gets. Upload a cover image, because buyers cannot hold a digital file, so the image does most of the selling. Set your price, then check the advanced settings.

Payhip lets you set a download limit for each file. The default is 3 download attempts per file, and you can change it. For PDF files, turn on PDF stamping, which prints the buyer's details on each page and discourages casual sharing. If you sell software, Payhip can generate licence keys for you, and since 2026 you can also upload your own pre-generated keys.

For pricing, look at what similar products sell for in your niche, then price for the result your product gives, not for the hours it took you to make it. A template that saves a buyer a day of work is worth more than its page count suggests. Our guide on how to price digital products walks through the maths in detail.

Worked example

Example only: these numbers are not typical results.

Imagine you sell a Notion template pack for 19 US dollars on the free plan, and a buyer pays by card through Stripe in the United States.

  • Sale price: 19.00 US dollars
  • Payhip fee at 5 percent: 0.95 US dollars
  • Stripe fee at 2.9 percent plus 0.30 US dollars: 0.55 plus 0.30, which is 0.85 US dollars
  • Total fees: 0.95 plus 0.85, which is 1.80 US dollars
  • You keep: 19.00 minus 1.80, which is 17.20 US dollars

If you sell 20 copies in a month, you keep about 344 US dollars before tax. If you sell 60 copies, the fees on the free plan reach about 108 US dollars, which is more than the 29 US dollar Plus plan plus its lower 2 percent fee, so that is roughly the point where upgrading starts to save you money. Recalculate this with your own price and your own country's card rates before you decide.

Step 5: Customise your store and connect your domain

Payhip gives you a drag and drop store builder with ready made themes. You can change colours, fonts, layout sections, and your logo without writing code. Keep it simple. A clean store with one clear product beats a busy store with ten half finished listings.

You can also build product landing pages inside Payhip, add a blog, and customise the checkout page with your own banner, logo, and colours. Since 2026 you can add custom CSS to the checkout as well.

If you already have a website, you do not have to move it. Payhip lets you embed buy buttons and product cards on your existing site. A buyer clicks the button on your site and the Payhip checkout opens over the page. That works well if you already run a blog or a WordPress site and you only want Payhip for the checkout and delivery.

Step 6: Launch, promote, and make your first sale

Before you announce anything, buy your own product once. Check that the checkout works, the payment clears, the download email arrives, and the file opens. This five minute test catches most launch day problems.

Then turn on the marketing tools already inside Payhip. Create a coupon code for your launch, set up the affiliate programme so others can promote your product for a commission you choose, and send an email to your buyer list through Payhip's built in email tool. If you sell on social media, our guide on how to sell digital products with Instagram shows how to send followers to a Payhip checkout link from your bio.

Do not expect sales on day one. Most new sellers get their first sale from people who already follow them, not from strangers finding the store. Share the product where your audience already is, answer questions in public, and collect an email list from every buyer so you can sell the next product to the same people. Our guide on how to sell digital products with email marketing explains how to build that list.

What it costs and what to expect

You can start on Payhip for no monthly cost. Your only cost is the 5 percent fee on each sale, plus the card processing fee. If you buy a domain, expect about 10 to 15 US dollars a year, though prices vary by registrar.

The time cost is larger than the money cost. Expect to spend a weekend creating a good first product, and a few hours setting up the store, the product page, and the launch posts. After that, each sale takes almost no time, because delivery is automatic.

Be honest with yourself about the timeline. Most sellers make no sales in the first week, then a small number of sales from their own audience over the first month. Building steady sales takes months of posting, collecting emails, and improving the product page. Payhip gives you the shop. It does not bring you customers.

Tax is the part beginners most often miss. Payhip calculates, collects, and remits VAT and sales tax on digital sales in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union, at no extra charge. That saves you real work. Even so, you still need to keep records of your income and check the tax rules in your own country, because rules differ by country. The UK government guidance on VAT rules for digital services explains how the place of supply rules work for digital products sold to consumers.

Common mistakes

  • Launching with no audience and expecting Payhip to bring buyers, when Payhip is a shop, not a marketplace with built in traffic.
  • Pricing by the hour it took to create the product, instead of pricing for the result it gives the buyer.
  • Writing a product description that lists features, when buyers need to know what outcome they get.
  • Skipping the test purchase, then finding out on launch day that the download link or the payment fails.
  • Setting the download limit to 1, which creates support emails from buyers who switch devices.
  • Paying for the Plus plan before the first sale, when the free plan costs nothing until you earn.
  • Selling a product you have not checked for copyright problems, such as templates that reuse someone else's design.
  • Ignoring tax records, then struggling at the end of the year to work out what you earned.

Risks and scams to watch for

Payhip itself is a legitimate platform, but the digital product space attracts scams aimed at new sellers. Be careful of anyone who offers to grow your Payhip store for a fee paid in advance, or who promises a set number of sales. No one can guarantee sales, and a real marketer charges for work done, not for a promise.

Watch for fake buyer emails that claim a payment failed and ask you to click a link or send the file directly. Real Payhip sales always show in your dashboard, so check there before you send anything. Never share your Payhip password, and never give a stranger access to your Stripe or PayPal account.

If you lose money to a scam, report it to your payment provider first, because they are the ones who may be able to reverse a payment. In the United States, you can also report fraud to the Federal Trade Commission, and the FTC explains how to report fraud on its consumer advice site. Readers outside the United States should use their own country's consumer protection body.

Payhip also has rules about what you can sell. Certain products are not permitted, and Payhip can remove a store that breaks its terms, so read the prohibited products list in the help centre before you list anything unusual. Frequently asked questions

Can I sell digital products on Payhip for free?

Yes. The free plan has no monthly fee and no limit on products or sales. You pay 5 percent of each sale to Payhip, plus your payment processor's fee, which is about 2.9 percent plus 0.30 US dollars for a standard United States card payment through Stripe. You only pay when you sell something.

How do I get paid on Payhip?

Payhip pays you through your connected payment gateway, usually Stripe or PayPal. The money lands in your Stripe or PayPal account as soon as the payment clears, rather than in a weekly or monthly payout. Make sure your gateway account is fully set up, with your bank details added, before you launch.

What digital products sell best on Payhip?

Templates, planners, presets, ebooks, short courses, and memberships sell well, because they solve a specific problem and are easy to deliver as a file. The best product is usually the one that matches a skill you already have and an audience you can already reach. A narrow product for a clear buyer beats a broad product for everyone.

Does Payhip handle VAT and sales tax?

Yes. Payhip calculates, collects, and remits VAT and sales tax on digital product sales in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. This is included on every plan at no extra charge. You still need to keep your own income records and check the tax rules in your own country.

Can I use my own domain with Payhip?

Yes. You can connect a custom domain to your Payhip store on any plan, including the free plan, and Payhip includes a free SSL certificate for it. You buy the domain from a registrar, then point it at Payhip following the steps in the dashboard. If you do not have a domain, Payhip gives you a free store address instead.

Is Payhip better than Gumroad?

It depends on what you value. Payhip charges 5 percent on its free plan and 0 percent on its Pro plan, while Gumroad charges a flat 10 percent plus a per sale fee. Payhip also includes courses, memberships, and coaching on every plan. Gumroad has a larger built in audience and a simpler checkout for some buyers. Compare the fees at your expected sales volume before you choose.

Conclusion

To sell digital products on Payhip, pick one clear product, create your free store, connect Stripe and PayPal, upload your file, set a price that reflects the result, and test the whole checkout before you launch. Start on the free plan, let Payhip handle delivery and digital tax, and spend your energy on reaching buyers and building your email list.
