Editorial policy

Our readers trust us to be accurate and independent. This policy explains how we earn that trust.

Accuracy

We check platform rules, fees, and requirements against official documentation wherever possible, and we date our guides so readers can see how current they are. Where information is an estimate or an opinion, we say so.

Independence

Advertisers and affiliate partners do not decide what we cover or what we conclude. A platform that pays us a commission can still receive a critical review.

No invented experience

We do not invent personal experiments, testimonials, or earnings results. If we have not used a platform ourselves, we say what our conclusion is based on — documentation, pricing pages, and independent reporting.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we correct it promptly and note the change. See our corrections policy.
