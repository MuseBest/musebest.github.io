How to Earn Money on Braintrust in 2026

If you have deep knowledge in a specialist field, you may be able to earn well without bidding on low paid gigs. Learning how to earn money on Braintrust is worth your time, because Braintrust charges freelancers no fee at all, and it now offers AI training work as well as normal freelance contracts.

Braintrust is a talent network that connects vetted professionals with companies. Its homepage says it gives clients access to more than 2 million vetted professionals across engineering, design, product, and operations, in more than 100 countries. Talent keeps 100% of the rate they agree, because clients pay a 15% fee on top.

This guide explains what Braintrust is, the two main ways to earn on it, how to pass its approval process, how pay works, and the risks you should know. All facts were checked on 15 November 2026.

What is Braintrust and how does it work

Braintrust, at usebraintrust.com, is a talent marketplace with a difference. On most freelance sites, the platform takes a cut of your pay. On Braintrust, talent pays no fee. The client pays a 15% fee on top of your rate. That structure is the main reason experienced freelancers look at Braintrust first.

There are two main ways to earn. The first is normal freelance and contract work: companies hire designers, developers, product managers, marketers, and other specialists for project or ongoing work. The second is AI training work: rating AI answers, labelling data, expert evaluation, red teaming, and code review. AI training work is flexible, with no minimum hours, and it suits people with real domain knowledge rather than AI skills. Braintrust says no AI background is needed for these tasks, because your field knowledge is the skill companies need.

AI training fields listed by Braintrust include healthcare and medicine, legal, software engineering, data science, finance, education, creative writing, and science and research. If you have three or more years of professional experience in one of these areas, you may qualify.

Braintrust also has its own token, called BTRST. You can earn tokens for joining, for referrals, and for helping the network. Tokens give you a vote on network decisions and access to premium features. They are not shares, and they do not pay a dividend. The token section below explains this in plain terms.

Step 1: Build a profile that shows real expertise

Braintrust only approves experienced professionals, so your profile must prove your depth. A vague profile is the most common reason for rejection.

Start with your specialisation. Pick the field where you have the most experience, and say it clearly in your headline. Braintrust says a new member can sign up in about 20 minutes, but a strong profile takes longer to prepare, so do not rush it.

List at least five skills, and mark your top three as Superpowers. These are the skills Braintrust uses to match you with tasks and pay levels, so choose the ones you can prove with real work.

Add your full work history, with years of experience for each role. Braintrust lists three or more years of professional experience among its approval criteria. Attach a detailed resume to your first application, because Braintrust says applications with a resume get noticed faster.

Write your experience as results. Say what you did, who it helped, and what changed. For example, a finance expert might say they built risk models for a bank. A doctor might say they reviewed clinical guidelines for a hospital group. Concrete detail beats general claims every time.

Step 2: Apply for your first job

Once your profile is complete, apply to a job that genuinely matches your experience. Your profile must be at 100% before you can apply, so finish every section first.

Pick your first application with care. Braintrust says applications with a high resume match score get noticed first. A strong match also triggers the approval process in the right order. If you apply to a role far outside your field, you may be rejected before a human ever reads your profile.

As of 15 November 2026, third-party trackers list 61 open Braintrust roles, with typical pay from $25 to $100 an hour and a median of $40. That figure covers the roles listed on that day, not all work on the platform, and it changes often. Use it as a rough guide only.

Step 3: Pass identity checks and the AI screening interview

Sending your first application triggers two checks. The first is identity verification. Braintrust runs it through a service called Persona. You need a government photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence, and you must take a selfie that matches it. You get two attempts in total, so take the photo in good light with a clear image. Your ID and selfie are not shared with clients.

The second check is a short screening interview with Braintrust's AI interviewer, called the AIR Screening Interviewer. Braintrust says it is not a technical test. It asks about your problem solving, your past work, your communication skills, your experience and impact, and your pay goals and work authorisation. The interview takes about 5 to 10 minutes, and you usually get one chance per question, so think before you answer.

Your application is typically reviewed within 24 hours, and within 72 hours if you apply on a Friday, weekend, or holiday. If you are approved, you get a badge marking you as Approved Talent, and your applications start being shown to clients. You can then message Talent Matchers and clients on the platform.

If you are not approved, Braintrust says you can go through screening again after six months. Use that time to build stronger proof of your experience. Common reasons for rejection include a thin resume, skills that do not match current client needs, weak communication, fewer than three years of experience, and not being able to start work right away.

Step 4: Work on contracts or AI training tasks

Once approved, you can browse jobs and AI training tasks on your dashboard. For AI training work, the process is simple: see the task, see the pay, and accept it if it fits. There are no minimum hours and no set schedule.

For contract work, you apply, interview with the client, and agree terms. Braintrust says the average engagement lasts about seven months, according to third-party reviews, which is longer than a typical gig. That suits people who want steady work rather than one-off jobs.

Do the work well and on time. Your ratings and your record on the platform affect which jobs you see next. Keep your communication clear, especially with clients in other time zones. If a task is unclear, ask before you start, because rework eats your hourly rate.

How you get paid on Braintrust

Braintrust pays talent in normal money, not in tokens, for the work itself. The table below sums up the pay side, based on Braintrust's own pages and independent guides read on 15 November 2026.

Item What we found
Talent fee 0%. You keep 100% of your rate
Client fee 15% on top of your rate, paid by the client
AI training pay, overall $20 to $200 an hour, by task type
AI answer rating work $25 to $60 an hour
Expert evaluation work $60 to $150 an hour
Code review work $75 to $200 an hour
Typical listed roles $25 to $100 an hour, median $40, per third-party tracker
Payment timing Weekly for AI training tasks. Contract work follows the invoice terms, often net 30
Payment method Wise and Stripe, into bank accounts in 35 countries as of 1 November 2026
Extra costs You may pay payment processor fees, such as withdrawal or currency conversion

Pay is shown before you accept a task, so you always know what a job pays before you start it. Braintrust guarantees no salary and no minimum payment. You are an independent contractor, not an employee, and you handle your own taxes.

One limit matters for readers outside the main payout countries. To be paid through Braintrust, you need a bank account in a country its payment partners Wise and Stripe support. As of 1 November 2026, that list had 35 countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and India. If your country is not supported, your job feed shows only roles where the client pays you directly. Check the current list before you apply.

The BTRST token, explained simply

BTRST is Braintrust's own token. It is used for governance, which means token holders can vote on network decisions, such as rules and fees. Braintrust's official guide to earning and using BTRST explains how tokens are earned and spent. You earn tokens for becoming Approved Talent, for referring new talent, and for referring new clients. You can also spend tokens on premium features, such as 1:1 career help and application boosts.

Referral rewards are paid in BTRST, not in cash. Braintrust says you earn 2% of every invoice paid by a company you refer, up to $100,000 in BTRST per company. For talent you refer, you earn 1% of every invoice they generate. Those are ceilings, not typical rewards. A $100,000 figure would need a very large client paying over a long time.

The minimum withdrawal is 10 BTRST. You can move tokens to an external wallet that supports the token standard, but Braintrust warns that once tokens leave the platform, they are your responsibility. If you lose them, the network cannot get them back.

Be careful with tokens. Braintrust itself says BTRST is not stock, not equity, and not a claim on profits or dividends. Its cash value can rise or fall, and tax rules for tokens differ by country. Do not count token rewards as income until you understand their value and your local tax rules. The real money on Braintrust is the fee-free pay for your work, not the token.

What it costs and what to expect

Joining Braintrust costs no money. The costs are your time and the effort of proving your experience.

Item Typical cost
Sign-up and profile $0, about 20 minutes to sign up, longer to do well
Identity verification $0, needs a government ID and a selfie
Screening interview $0, about 5 to 10 minutes
Review wait Usually 24 hours, up to 72 hours
Platform fee on your pay $0
Payment processor fees Varies by country, currency, and bank
Total cash cost $0, plus any processor fees on withdrawal

Expect a slow start. Approval takes a day or three, and then you must find tasks that match your field. AI training tasks can start fast once you are approved, because there is no long hiring process. Contract roles take longer, because clients interview first.

Example only: what a month could look like

This is a labelled example, not a typical result. It shows the maths only.

An expert takes evaluation tasks at $90 an hour for 10 hours a week. That is $900 a week. Over four weeks, that is $3,600 gross. Braintrust takes no fee, so the gross stays $3,600. If the expert sets aside 20% for tax, that is $720, which leaves $2,880 before processor fees and other costs.

The same expert at $30 an hour for the same hours would gross $1,200 in the month. The task type and your pay grade matter far more than the hours you put in. That is why your specialisation and your interview matter so much.

Pros and cons of Braintrust

Pros

  • You keep 100% of your rate, with no talent fee.
  • Clients pay a 15% fee on top, so the cost is transparent.
  • AI training work is flexible, with no minimum hours.
  • Pay is shown before you accept a task.
  • Payments for AI training tasks are processed weekly.
  • Contract roles can last months, not days.

Cons

  • You need three or more years of professional experience.
  • Approval can be refused, and re-screening takes six months.
  • Payout through Braintrust covers only 35 countries as of November 2026.
  • Contract pay follows invoice terms, which can mean waiting for payment.
  • The token adds confusion, and its cash value is not stable.
  • Braintrust guarantees no salary and no minimum payment.

Who Braintrust suits and who it does not suit

Braintrust suits experienced professionals with deep knowledge in a field clients need: doctors, lawyers, engineers, data scientists, finance experts, teachers, and senior designers, marketers, and product managers. It suits people who can prove their experience and who want flexible, well paid work without a platform fee.

It also suits experts who want AI training work on the side. If you can judge good answers in your field, rate AI output, or review code, you can earn without a long hiring process.

Braintrust does not suit beginners, students, or people with general skills and no specialism. It does not suit people who need a guaranteed salary or who cannot pass an identity check. It does not suit people in countries outside its payout list who need payment through the platform itself. If you want to build your skills first, start with how to learn data analysis online or a similar guide, and return when you have proof of your expertise.

Common mistakes

  • Applying with a thin profile and no detailed resume.
  • Picking a first job that does not match your real experience.
  • Rushing the identity check with a blurry photo, then losing an attempt.
  • Treating the AI interview like a casual chat instead of a real interview.
  • Counting BTRST token rewards as cash income.
  • Accepting tasks below your skill level because they are easy to get.
  • Starting work in a country Braintrust cannot pay into, without checking first.
  • Forgetting to set aside money for tax, because you are a contractor.

Risks and scams to watch for

Braintrust is a legitimate network, but scammers copy real platforms, so stay alert.

Never pay anyone to join Braintrust, to pass screening, or to unlock tasks. Joining is free. If someone asks for a fee, it is a scam.

Watch for fake recruiters who message you outside the platform and ask you to move to chat apps, share bank details, or buy a starter kit. Real Braintrust contact happens through the platform or its official help centre. Braintrust says its team will never message you to ask for personal information or to discuss the value of BTRST.

Be careful with the token. Never share your wallet keys with anyone. If someone promises huge token rewards for a small payment, walk away. Token rewards are small for most people, and the big figures you see online are ceilings, not typical payouts.

Also remember that you are a contractor. If a client does not pay an invoice on time, you must follow the platform process and keep records. Keep copies of your contracts, invoices, and messages. Do not work off the platform to avoid fees, because Braintrust's terms prohibit it and you would lose payment protection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Braintrust legit?

Yes. Braintrust is a real talent network with large enterprise clients, and it pays talent in normal money through Wise and Stripe. It charges talent no fee. That said, approval is not guaranteed, and Braintrust promises no salary and no minimum payment. Treat it as a marketplace, not as a job offer.

How much can I earn on Braintrust?

It depends on the task and your field. Braintrust's AI training page lists $20 to $200 an hour by task type, with answer rating at $25 to $60, expert evaluation at $60 to $150, and code review at $75 to $200 an hour. A third-party tracker lists typical open roles at $25 to $100 an hour, with a median of $40. Your actual pay depends on your specialisation, your pay grade, and how many tasks you take.

Do I need AI experience to work on Braintrust?

No. For AI training work, Braintrust says no AI background is needed. What matters is real expertise in your field, such as medicine, law, finance, or software engineering. You use that knowledge to judge AI answers, not to build AI systems.

How do I get paid on Braintrust?

AI training tasks are paid weekly, and the pay is shown before you accept a task. Contract work follows the invoice terms, which are often net 30. Braintrust pays through Wise and Stripe into bank accounts in 35 countries as of 1 November 2026. You may still pay processor fees for withdrawal or currency conversion.

What is the BTRST token worth?

BTRST is a governance token, not cash and not shares. You earn it for joining, for referrals, and for helping the network. You can spend it on premium features or move it to an external wallet, with a minimum withdrawal of 10 BTRST. Its cash value changes over time, and tax rules differ by country. Do not treat token rewards as reliable income.

How long does Braintrust approval take?

Your application is typically reviewed within 24 hours, and within 72 hours if you apply on a Friday, weekend, or holiday. The screening interview itself takes about 5 to 10 minutes. If you are not approved, you can try screening again after six months.

Conclusion

Braintrust is worth a serious look if you have three or more years of experience in a specialist field. You keep 100% of your rate, AI training work is flexible, and pay is shown before you accept a task. The trade-offs are a strict approval process, payout limits in some countries, and no guaranteed income.

If that fits you, build a detailed profile, attach a strong resume, apply to a role that matches your field, and take the screening interview seriously. Ignore the token hype and judge the platform by the pay for real work.
