2b:T4d19,<h2 id="how-to-earn-money-on-toptal-in-2026">How to Earn Money on Toptal in 2026</h2>
<p>Learning how to earn money on Toptal starts with one honest fact: Toptal is not a marketplace for beginners. It is a vetted network for experienced professionals, and it accepts only a small share of applicants. If you are a senior developer, designer, finance expert or project manager with several years of real client work behind you, it can be one of the highest paying ways to freelance. If you are just starting out, your time is better spent building experience elsewhere first.</p>
<p>This guide is for mid to senior professionals who want enterprise clients without bidding for every job. You will learn how Toptal works, what the screening involves, what rates look like, how matching and payment work, and how to decide whether applying is worth your time.</p>

<h3 id="what-toptal-is-and-who-it-suits">What Toptal is and who it suits</h3>
<p>Toptal, short for top talent, is a freelance network that connects experienced professionals with large companies and funded startups. It was founded in 2010 and it markets itself as a network of the top 3 percent of freelance talent worldwide. That 3 percent figure is Toptal's own claim, repeated by guides, and it tells you how selective the platform is.</p>
<p>Toptal covers five main areas: software development, design, finance, project management and product management. Its clients include large enterprises and well known tech companies. The work is usually longer engagements of several months, not one off gigs, and most of it is remote.</p>
<p>Toptal suits you if you have at least three to five years of deep experience in one of those areas, you can explain technical decisions clearly in English, and you want steady client work without writing proposals. It does not suit you if you are a junior freelancer, if you need income this month, or if you want to pick up small quick jobs. For a gentler starting point, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/earning-platforms/how-to-make-money-on-upwork" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to make money on Upwork</a> or <a href="https://earnquill.com/earning-platforms/how-to-make-money-on-fiverr" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to make money on Fiverr</a>, where anyone can create a profile and start.</p>
<h3 id="how-earning-on-toptal-works">How earning on Toptal works</h3>
<p>Toptal works differently from open marketplaces. You do not bid on jobs. Once you pass screening, a Talent Coordinator, sometimes called a matcher, reviews open client briefs and introduces you to clients who fit your skills. You then have a short client interview, and if both sides agree, the engagement starts.</p>
<p>The most important money fact is this: Toptal does not take a cut from the hourly rate you set. If you agree a rate of 80 US dollars an hour, you receive 80 US dollars an hour. Toptal makes its money by adding a markup on top of your rate and billing that higher figure to the client. This is confirmed on <a href="https://www.toptal.com/freelance-jobs/developers/software" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Toptal's own jobs pages</a>, checked in November 2026.</p>

<h3 id="step-1-check-you-are-ready">Step 1: Check you are ready</h3>
<p>Do not apply on impulse. A failed application carries a cooldown period, typically 2 to 12 months depending on the stage you failed, during which you cannot reapply. Use this checklist before you submit anything.</p>
<p>First, count your real experience. Toptal's screening assumes senior level skill. If you have under three years of professional work, or your experience is mostly tutorials and personal projects, wait and build a stronger record first.</p>
<p>Second, test your English. The first screening stage is a video call in English, and many strong technical candidates fail here because they cannot explain their thinking clearly. Record yourself explaining a project you built, then listen back. If you hesitate, ramble, or lean on jargon, practise explaining the same project in simple sentences until it flows.</p>
<p>Third, gather proof of your work. Clean up your GitHub, LinkedIn and portfolio. Screeners look at commit history, deployed projects and the complexity of what you claim to have built. One polished, deployed project with clear documentation beats ten half finished repositories. For portfolio help, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/freelancing/how-to-build-a-freelance-brand" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to build a freelance brand</a>.</p>
<p>Fourth, clear your calendar. Guides report the full process takes 2 to 8 weeks, and the test project alone can take 30 to 50 hours over 10 to 14 days. If you cannot give it that time, do not start yet.</p>
<h3 id="step-2-apply-and-prepare-your-profile">Step 2: Apply and prepare your profile</h3>
<p>Apply on <a href="https://www.toptal.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Toptal's website</a> with your resume and your strongest work samples. Put your main skill at the top of your resume, with the tools and technologies you use every day, not a long list of everything you have touched once.</p>
<p>Your profile should read like a senior professional's record, not a job application. Lead with outcomes, not duties. Instead of "worked on a web app", write what the app did, who used it, and what your part was. If you led a migration, fixed a slow system, or shipped a product used by real customers, say so in one or two sentences each.</p>
<p>Choose one clear specialism. A developer who lists ten languages looks unfocused next to a developer who is clearly a React specialist with backend experience. Toptal matches people to specific briefs, and a sharp profile is easier to match than a broad one.</p>
<p>While you wait for a response, prepare for the technical stages. Developers should practise timed algorithm problems and system design questions. Designers should prepare case studies that show the problem, the process and the result, not just finished screens. Finance experts should be ready to build a model live and talk through it.</p>

Second, do not use AI tools to write code during live interviews. Guides report that Toptal monitors screens and that using an AI copilot during an interview leads to disqualification. Third, if you fail any stage, the application ends and the cooldown begins, so treat every stage as if it is the only chance you get.</p>
<h3 id="step-4-set-your-rate">Step 4: Set your rate</h3>
<p>You set your own hourly rate during onboarding, in discussion with Toptal. The rate you set is the rate you receive in full, because Toptal adds its markup on top for the client.</p>
<p>Guides suggest senior freelancers start in the 70 to 100 US dollar an hour range and raise it after two successful engagements, rather than aiming for the ceiling on day one. That advice is sensible. Your first two engagements build your record inside Toptal, and a strong record earns you better matches later.</p>
<p>Think about your rate in monthly terms before you agree it. At 80 US dollars an hour and 30 billable hours a week, a four week month is about 9,600 US dollars before your own costs, taxes and payment provider fees. But engagements can have gaps between them, and you are not paid for time spent waiting for a match, so do not plan your budget as if every week is fully booked. For help with pricing generally, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/freelancing/how-to-raise-freelance-rates" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to raise freelance rates</a>.</p>
<h3 id="step-5-work-with-your-matcher-and-keep-clients">Step 5: Work with your matcher and keep clients</h3>
<p>Once you are in, your matcher is your most important relationship on the platform. Keep your availability current, answer quickly when a brief arrives, and be honest about whether a project fits you.</p>
<p>On your first engagement, over communicate. Confirm the scope in writing, send a short daily update, and flag risks before they become problems. Toptal monitors trial engagements closely, and a client complaint about communication or speed in the first weeks can end your place in the network.</p>
<p> Many Toptal engagements run for 6 to 12 months, and a happy client may extend or ask for you again. Ask for feedback at the end of each month, and act on it. That is how you move from one off matches to a steady flow of work.</p>
<h3 id="worked-example-what-a-toptal-engagement-could-pay">Worked example: what a Toptal engagement could pay</h3>
<p>Example only: these numbers are not typical results. They show how the maths works, so you can test your own figures.</p>
<p>Imagine a senior developer who passes screening and agrees a rate of 80 US dollars an hour. She works on an engagement for 30 billable hours a week for 12 weeks. That is 30 times 80, which is 2,400 US dollars a week, and 2,400 times 12 is 28,800 US dollars for the engagement. Toptal takes no cut from her rate, so she receives the full 28,800 US dollars before her own costs.</p>
<p>Now subtract the real costs. Payment provider and conversion fees of about 2 percent take about 576 US dollars. Tax depends on her country and is her own responsibility. The time she spent on screening, about 40 hours of preparation and testing, was unpaid, and the two weeks between applying and starting earned her nothing. Spread over the engagement, her effective rate is lower than 80 US dollars an hour once that unpaid time is counted.</p>
<p>Recheck: 30 times 80 is 2,400. 2,400 times 12 is 28,800. 2 percent of 28,800 is 576. 28,800 minus 576 is 28,224. The maths checks out.</p>

<h3 id="what-it-costs-and-what-to-expect">What it costs and what to expect</h3>
<p>Applying to Toptal costs no money. There is no application fee and no membership fee for freelancers. The cost is time: guides report 2 to 8 weeks from application to acceptance, including weeks of preparation and a test project that can take 30 to 50 hours.</p>
<p>Expect a slow start even after acceptance. Matching depends on client demand for your exact skills, and some new members wait weeks for their first introduction.</p>
<p>Results vary with your skill, your specialism, client demand, and how well you communicate. Toptal can pay far more than open marketplaces, but only for a small share of freelancers who pass a hard filter.</p>
<h3 id="common-mistakes">Common mistakes</h3>
<ul>
<li>Applying before you have three years of real professional experience, then hitting the cooldown period.</li>
<li>Treating the first screening call like a casual chat instead of a formal interview.</li>
<li>Spending too long perfecting the first problem in a timed test and leaving the rest unfinished.</li>
<li>Setting a rate at the top of the reported range on day one, then getting no matches.</li>
<li>Ignoring your matcher for weeks and expecting introductions to keep coming.</li>
<li>Using AI tools during live interviews, which guides report leads to disqualification.</li>
<li>Assuming acceptance means instant work, instead of planning for a wait between engagements.</li>
<li>Forgetting that you are responsible for your own taxes, because Toptal does not deduct them for you.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="risks-and-scams-to-watch-for">Risks and scams to watch for</h3>
<p>Toptal itself is a legitimate platform, but its name attracts scammers. No one from Toptal will ask you to pay an application fee, buy a starter kit, or send money to secure a place. Any message that asks for payment to join is a scam, even if it uses Toptal's logo. Apply only through <a href="https://www.toptal.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Toptal's own website</a>.</p>
<p>Be careful with the contract terms too. Guides report that Toptal contracts restrict taking a Toptal client off the platform without paying a buyout fee. Read your agreement before you sign, and do not promise a client direct work outside Toptal unless you understand what it costs.</p>
<p>Finally, protect your time. The screening process is a real investment of unpaid hours. If you are not ready, that time is better spent on paid work elsewhere, such as <a href="https://earnquill.com/earning-platforms/how-to-earn-money-on-contra" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Contra</a>, which has no screening test, or on building your portfolio for a later application.</p>
<h3 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h3>
<h4>How much can you earn on Toptal?</h4>
<p>Guides report hourly rates of 60 to 150 US dollars for developers and up to 200 for finance experts, with no commission taken from your rate. Your actual income depends on your rate, your billed hours, and gaps between engagements, and most applicants never pass screening.</p>
<h4>Is Toptal legit?</h4>
<p>Yes. Toptal is a real company founded in 2010, and it works with large enterprise clients. It does not charge freelancers to join, and it does not take a cut from your agreed rate. Its screening is strict, and about 97 percent of applicants are rejected according to guides.</p>
<h4>How hard is it to get into Toptal?</h4>
<p>Very hard. Toptal claims a 3 percent acceptance rate. The process has five stages, takes 2 to 8 weeks, and includes a test project that can take 30 to 50 hours. Junior freelancers and weak English speakers are usually rejected early.</p>
<h4>Does Toptal take a cut of your pay?</h4>
<p>No. Toptal states on its own FAQ pages that it does not take a cut from the hourly rate you set. It adds a markup on top of your rate and bills that to the client. You still pay your own payment provider and conversion fees, and your own taxes.</p>
<h4>Is the Toptal test project paid?</h4>
<p>Guides disagree. One guide describes it as unpaid work taking 30 to 50 hours, while another describes it as a paid project of 2 to 4 hours. We could not confirm the current position from Toptal's public pages, so confirm the terms when you reach that stage and plan as if it takes serious unpaid time.</p>
<h4>How long does Toptal screening take?</h4>
<p>Guides report 2 to 8 weeks from application to acceptance, depending on how fast you book each stage and whether you pass first time. Preparation before applying can add another month or two, and most guides recommend 8 to 12 weeks of preparation.</p>
<h3 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h3>
<p>To earn money on Toptal, build three to five years of real professional experience first, prepare your English and your technical skills for weeks, apply with a sharp specialist profile, pass five screening stages, set a realistic rate, and work closely with your matcher. The pay can be far higher than open marketplaces, and Toptal takes no cut from your rate, but the filter is strict and the unpaid preparation is real.</p>
