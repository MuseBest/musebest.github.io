41:T4c5d,<h1>How to Earn Money on SolidGigs in 2026</h1>
<p>Learning how to earn money on SolidGigs starts with correcting one common mistake. SolidGigs is not a marketplace where clients hire you. You cannot earn money directly on SolidGigs, because no hiring or payment happens there. SolidGigs is a paid lead service. You pay a monthly subscription, and SolidGigs sends you curated freelance job leads, helps you pitch for them, and, on higher plans, builds you a website and handles outreach. You earn when you win a client from those leads and do the work. This guide explains how that works in 2026, what it costs, and whether it is worth it.</p>

<h2 id="what-solidgigs-is-and-how-to-earn-money-on-solidgigs">What SolidGigs is, and how to earn money on SolidGigs</h2>
<p>SolidGigs is a subscription service for freelancers who want steady client work. It finds freelance job leads from across the web, filters them for quality, and delivers the ones that match your skills to your inbox every weekday. You then pitch for the leads you want, agree terms directly with the client, and keep 100% of what the client pays.</p>
<p>That distinction matters. On Upwork or Fiverr, the platform sits between you and the client, holds the payment, and takes a fee. On SolidGigs, there is no platform between you and the client once you have the lead. You work directly with the client and agree your own price.</p>
<p>SolidGigs is also not a job board. A job board lists every job it can find, and you must search through them yourself. SolidGigs curates its leads, which means it reviews jobs and sends you only the ones that match the skills and client type you chose when you signed up. That saves time, but you still have to pitch for every lead. No lead is a guaranteed job.</p>
<h2 id="how-solidgigs-works-in-five-steps">How SolidGigs works in five steps</h2>
<p>The process follows the same pattern every week.</p>
<ol>
<li>Sign up and set your filters. You answer questions about your skills, your niche, and the kind of clients you want.</li>
<li>Receive leads every weekday, matched to your filters.</li>
<li>Review the leads and choose the ones worth pitching.</li>
<li>Pitch fast with a tailored message, using the AI generated pitches as a starting point.</li>
<li>Win the client and work directly with them. You agree the price, do the work, and keep 100% of the payment.</li>
</ol>
<p>On higher plans, SolidGigs also builds you a portfolio website and handles some marketing and outreach. That work runs in the background while you pitch, but it does not replace pitching. The leads still need a fast, personal response from you.</p>

<h2 id="the-90-day-guarantee-explained-honestly">The 90 day guarantee, explained honestly</h2>
<p>SolidGigs offers a 90 day new client guarantee on all plans, according to the pricing section of <a href="https://solidgigs.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">its homepage</a>. The wording is clear. If you follow the SolidGigs system and do not get a new client within 90 days, SolidGigs will keep working for free until you do. <p>First, the guarantee promises more work from SolidGigs, not a refund and not a client. If you do not win a client in 90 days, you get extra months of the service at no cost, not your money back. Second, the guarantee depends on following the SolidGigs system, which means using the leads, sending pitches, and doing the work the service asks of you. If you sign up and never pitch, the guarantee is unlikely to apply. Third, one answer on the site's own FAQ page describes the guarantee in terms of the Pro and Expert plans, while the pricing section states that it applies to all three. If the guarantee matters to your decision, confirm which plans it covers with SolidGigs before you pay.</p>

The Pipeline plan gives up to 100 opportunities a month, and higher plans give unlimited opportunities. The number that matters is how many leads fit you well enough to pitch. A lead for a skill you do not offer is not useful, however many arrive.</p>

SolidGigs generates a draft pitch for each lead, and the Pipeline plan includes 10 a month while the higher plans include unlimited pitches. The site itself advises you to personalise the pitch before you send it, and that advice is right. A generic pitch will lose to a short message that mentions the client's project by name and explains why you fit it. Use the AI draft to save time, then rewrite the opening and the closing in your own words.</p>

Your website goes live in less than 14 days, according to the site. The Pro plan includes 3 website updates a month and 1 new case study a month, while Expert includes unlimited updates and case studies. After 12 months, you own your website outright, according to the site's FAQ page.</p>

That is a lot of marketing help, but at 839 US dollars a month billed annually, it only makes sense if your freelance income is already high enough.</p>
<h2 id="how-to-earn-money-on-solidgigs-and-make-the-subscription-pay">How to earn money on SolidGigs and make the subscription pay</h2>
<p>A subscription only pays if the work it brings is worth more than its cost. Start with the 14 day free trial, and use it properly. Set your filters with care on day one, and pitch for the best leads at once. Do not spend the trial browsing. The trial is your chance to test whether the leads fit your skills, and you can only learn that by pitching.</p>
<p>Track your numbers from the first week. Note how many leads arrive, how many you pitch for, and how many turn into paid work. Then do the maths. On the Pipeline plan at 29 US dollars a month, one small project a month usually covers the cost many times over. On the Pro plan at 99 US dollars a month, you need more work to cover it. On the Expert plan at 839 US dollars a month, you need a large and steady income.</p>
<p>Speed matters more than volume. Opportunities move fast, and members who respond on the same day land work faster than those who wait. Check your leads each morning, and send a tailored pitch the same day. Follow up once if you hear nothing, then move on. Keep your portfolio ready, so a client who replies can see your work at once.</p>

Check the cancellation terms in your account before the trial ends, because this guide could not confirm whether the trial needs a card or the full refund terms beyond the guarantee wording.</p>
<p>Watch for scams that target freelancers looking for leads. Be wary of anyone who asks you to pay a fee outside the official site, asks for your bank details by message, or promises guaranteed work for a fee. Real leads come through your SolidGigs account, and real clients discuss the project before they discuss payment.
