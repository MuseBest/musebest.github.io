<h2 id="what-is-braintrust-and-how-does-it-work">What is Braintrust and how does it work</h2>
<p>Braintrust, at <a href="https://www.usebraintrust.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">usebraintrust.com</a>, is a talent marketplace with a difference. On most freelance sites, the platform takes a cut of your pay. On Braintrust, talent pays no fee. The client pays a 15% fee on top of your rate. That structure is the main reason experienced freelancers look at Braintrust first.</p>
<p>There are two main ways to earn. The first is normal freelance and contract work: companies hire designers, developers, product managers, marketers, and other specialists for project or ongoing work. The second is AI training work: rating AI answers, labelling data, expert evaluation, red teaming, and code review. AI training work is flexible, with no minimum hours, and it suits people with real domain knowledge rather than AI skills. Braintrust says no AI background is needed for these tasks, because your field knowledge is the skill companies need.</p>
<p>AI training fields listed by Braintrust include healthcare and medicine, legal, software engineering, data science, finance, education, creative writing, and science and research. If you have three or more years of professional experience in one of these areas, you may qualify.</p>
<p>Braintrust also has its own token, called BTRST. You can earn tokens for joining, for referrals, and for helping the network. Tokens give you a vote on network decisions and access to premium features. They are not shares, and they do not pay a dividend. The token section below explains this in plain terms.</p>
<h2 id="step-1-build-a-profile-that-shows-real-expertise">Step 1: Build a profile that shows real expertise</h2>
<p>Braintrust only approves experienced professionals, so your profile must prove your depth. A vague profile is the most common reason for rejection.</p>
<p>Start with your specialisation. Pick the field where you have the most experience, and say it clearly in your headline. Braintrust says a new member can sign up in about 20 minutes, but a strong profile takes longer to prepare, so do not rush it.</p>
<p>List at least five skills, and mark your top three as Superpowers. These are the skills Braintrust uses to match you with tasks and pay levels, so choose the ones you can prove with real work.</p>
<p>Add your full work history, with years of experience for each role. Braintrust lists three or more years of professional experience among its approval criteria. Attach a detailed resume to your first application, because Braintrust says applications with a resume get noticed faster.</p>
<p>Write your experience as results. Say what you did, who it helped, and what changed. For example, a finance expert might say they built risk models for a bank. A doctor might say they reviewed clinical guidelines for a hospital group. Concrete detail beats general claims every time.</p>
<h2 id="step-2-apply-for-your-first-job">Step 2: Apply for your first job</h2>
<p>Once your profile is complete, apply to a job that genuinely matches your experience. Your profile must be at 100% before you can apply, so finish every section first.</p>
<p>Pick your first application with care. Braintrust says applications with a high resume match score get noticed first. A strong match also triggers the approval process in the right order. If you apply to a role far outside your field, you may be rejected before a human ever reads your profile.</p>
<p>As of 15 November 2026, third-party trackers list 61 open Braintrust roles, with typical pay from $25 to $100 an hour and a median of $40. That figure covers the roles listed on that day, not all work on the platform, and it changes often. Use it as a rough guide only.</p>
<h2 id="step-3-pass-identity-checks-and-the-ai-screening-interview">Step 3: Pass identity checks and the AI screening interview</h2>
<p>Sending your first application triggers two checks. The first is identity verification. Braintrust runs it through a service called Persona. You need a government photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence, and you must take a selfie that matches it. You get two attempts in total, so take the photo in good light with a clear image. Your ID and selfie are not shared with clients.</p>
<p>The second check is a short screening interview with Braintrust's AI interviewer, called the AIR Screening Interviewer. Braintrust says it is not a technical test. It asks about your problem solving, your past work, your communication skills, your experience and impact, and your pay goals and work authorisation. The interview takes about 5 to 10 minutes, and you usually get one chance per question, so think before you answer.</p>
<p>Your application is typically reviewed within 24 hours, and within 72 hours if you apply on a Friday, weekend, or holiday. If you are approved, you get a badge marking you as Approved Talent, and your applications start being shown to clients. You can then message Talent Matchers and clients on the platform.</p>
<p>If you are not approved, Braintrust says you can go through screening again after six months. Use that time to build stronger proof of your experience. Common reasons for rejection include a thin resume, skills that do not match current client needs, weak communication, fewer than three years of experience, and not being able to start work right away.</p>

<h2 id="step-4-work-on-contracts-or-ai-training-tasks">Step 4: Work on contracts or AI training tasks</h2>
<p>Once approved, you can browse jobs and AI training tasks on your dashboard. For AI training work, the process is simple: see the task, see the pay, and accept it if it fits. There are no minimum hours and no set schedule.</p>
<p>For contract work, you apply, interview with the client, and agree terms. Braintrust says the average engagement lasts about seven months, according to third-party reviews, which is longer than a typical gig. That suits people who want steady work rather than one-off jobs.</p>
<p>Do the work well and on time. Your ratings and your record on the platform affect which jobs you see next. Keep your communication clear, especially with clients in other time zones. If a task is unclear, ask before you start, because rework eats your hourly rate.</p>

<h2 id="the-btrst-token-explained-simply">The BTRST token, explained simply</h2>
<p>BTRST is Braintrust's own token. It is used for governance, which means token holders can vote on network decisions, such as rules and fees. You earn tokens for becoming Approved Talent, for referring new talent, and for referring new clients. You can also spend tokens on premium features, such as 1:1 career help and application boosts.</p>
<p>Referral rewards are paid in BTRST, not in cash. Braintrust says you earn 2% of every invoice paid by a company you refer, up to $100,000 in BTRST per company. For talent you refer, you earn 1% of every invoice they generate. Those are ceilings, not typical rewards. A $100,000 figure would need a very large client paying over a long time.</p>
<p>The minimum withdrawal is 10 BTRST. You can move tokens to an external wallet that supports the token standard, but Braintrust warns that once tokens leave the platform, they are your responsibility. If you lose them, the network cannot get them back.</p>
<p>Be careful with tokens. Braintrust itself says BTRST is not stock, not equity, and not a claim on profits or dividends. Its cash value can rise or fall, and tax rules for tokens differ by country. Do not count token rewards as income until you understand their value and your local tax rules. The real money on Braintrust is the fee-free pay for your work, not the token.</p>

<h2 id="risks-and-scams-to-watch-for">Risks and scams to watch for</h2>
<p>Braintrust is a legitimate network, but scammers copy real platforms, so stay alert.</p>
<p>Never pay anyone to join Braintrust, to pass screening, or to unlock tasks. Joining is free. If someone asks for a fee, it is a scam.</p>
<p>Watch for fake recruiters who message you outside the platform and ask you to move to chat apps, share bank details, or buy a starter kit. Real Braintrust contact happens through the platform or its official help centre. Braintrust says its team will never message you to ask for personal information or to discuss the value of BTRST.</p>
<p>Be careful with the token. Never share your wallet keys with anyone. If someone promises huge token rewards for a small payment, walk away. Token rewards are small for most people, and the big figures you see online are ceilings, not typical payouts.</p>
<p>Also remember that you are a contractor. If a client does not pay an invoice on time, you must follow the platform process and keep records. Keep copies of your contracts, invoices, and messages. Do not work off the platform to avoid fees, because Braintrust's terms prohibit it and you would lose payment protection.</p>

A third-party tracker lists typical open roles at $25 to $100 an hour, with a median of $40. Your actual pay depends on your specialisation, your pay grade, and how many tasks you take.</p>

<h3 id="how-do-i-get-paid-on-braintrust">How do I get paid on Braintrust?</h3>

You can spend it on premium features or move it to an external wallet, with a minimum withdrawal of 10 BTRST. Its cash value changes over time, and tax rules differ by country.
