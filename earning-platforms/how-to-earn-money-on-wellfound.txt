<p>Wellfound is a job marketplace for startup and tech jobs. It was formerly known as AngelList Talent. Wellfound official <a href="https://wellfound.com/about-us" rel="noopener" target="_blank">about page</a> confirms that AngelList Talent spun out as an independent company and rebranded as Wellfound in November 2022, with profiles, jobs and connections carried over. AngelList continues separately, focused on venture and fund work. So if an older guide mentions AngelList Talent, it means the same marketplace under its old name.</p>
<p>Wellfound says more than 27,000 companies hire on the platform, with more than 10 million candidates and more than 100,000 hires made. Treat those as platform claims. Jobs cover engineering, product, design, marketing, sales and operations, and the board is strongest for early stage companies from seed to Series B.</p>
<p>Wellfound is not a freelance marketplace. On a marketplace such as Upwork or Fiverr, clients post projects, freelancers bid or sell services, and the platform holds the payment in escrow and takes a fee. On Wellfound, startups post jobs, you apply with your profile, and responses come directly from founders and hiring managers. There is no bidding, no fee on your pay, and no escrow. Freelancers use Wellfound to find longer contract and remote roles with startups, not gig by gig work.</p>
<p>Wellfound is not a recruitment agency. Nobody there applies for you. You still have to apply, interview and agree terms yourself. Companies post jobs for free and pay for optional extras. Job seekers browse and apply for free. If you are weighing up platform types, read <a href="https://earnquill.com/earning-platforms/how-to-choose-earning-platform" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to choose an earning platform</a>.</p>
<h2 id="how-to-earn-money-on-wellfound-in-7-steps">How to earn money on Wellfound in 7 steps</h2>
<p>Follow these steps in order. They take about an hour to set up, and then a short check a few times a week.</p>
<h3 id="step-1-create-a-free-profile-and-complete-every-field">Step 1: Create a free profile and complete every field</h3>
<p>Create a free profile, because your profile works as your CV and your application. Add your work history, skills, education, projects and links to your work. Upload your CV so Wellfound can fill in key details. Show results, not only job titles. For example, write that you grew a store from zero to 5,000 monthly visitors, rather than only naming your job title. If you need help with the CV itself, use a clean single column layout with clear headings and no graphics.</p>
<h3 id="step-2-set-your-preferences-before-you-apply">Step 2: Set your preferences before you apply</h3>
<p>Set your preferences up front: salary range, equity minimum, industries, company sizes, funding stages and remote preference. Equity means a share of ownership in the company, usually as stock options that may become valuable or may be worth nothing. Clear preferences help the platform match you to better roles.</p>
<p><img src="/covers/ep31-wellfound-profile.jpg" alt="A person updating an online professional profile on a laptop with portfolio printouts on the desk" /></p>
<h3 id="step-3-mark-yourself-as-open-to-work">Step 3: Mark yourself as open to work</h3>
<p>Set your profile status so recruiters can find you, using statuses such as Open to Offers or Ready to Interview. A hidden profile gets few messages. Keep your status current, and turn it off when you are no longer looking.</p>
<h3 id="step-4-browse-jobs-or-wait-to-be-discovered">Step 4: Browse jobs or wait to be discovered</h3>
<p>You can actively apply to jobs, or wait for companies to reach out if your profile matches their needs. Most roles let you apply with one click using your profile, without a custom cover letter. Read each listing in full before you apply. Check the salary and equity shown, the location and remote policy, the company stage, and whether the role is full time, part time or contract. Put your fit in the first two lines of any note, and quantify your impact where you can.</p>
<h3 id="step-5-apply-to-a-short-well-matched-list-each-week">Step 5: Apply to a short, well matched list each week</h3>
<p>Apply to a focused list of roles that truly fit, rather than sending your profile to every startup. Early stage startups often value generalists who can do many things, while later stage startups hire specialists. If you are a beginner, target early stage companies where a broad skill set helps. Keep a simple list of applications, dates and replies. Check your applied list often, because unanswered applications expire after about two weeks with no company action.</p>
<h3 id="step-6-respond-fast-and-prepare-for-startup-interviews">Step 6: Respond fast and prepare for startup interviews</h3>
<p>Responses come directly from founders and hiring managers, not third party recruiters. Reply promptly and professionally. Some companies ask for follow up tasks or tests, such as improving sample content or completing a short exercise. Test your camera, microphone and internet connection before video calls, and prepare short answers about your results, your availability and your time zone. Founders check references and portfolios, so keep your profile honest.</p>
<p><img src="/covers/ep31-wellfound-interview.jpg" alt="A job candidate in a friendly video interview with a startup founder on a laptop screen" /></p>
<h3 id="step-7-agree-pay-equity-and-terms-in-writing-before-you-start">Step 7: Agree pay, equity and terms in writing before you start</h3>
<p>Before you start work, agree the salary or contract rate, the pay schedule, the currency, the payment method and the hours in writing. If equity is part of the offer, ask what type it is, how much you get, the vesting schedule, and what happens if you leave. Vesting means earning your equity over time, often over four years with a one year cliff. Get the terms in writing and take independent advice if the equity part is large.</p>

<h2 id="how-you-get-paid">How you get paid</h2>
<p>Wellfound does not pay you, and it takes no fee from your earnings. The startup pays you directly, so your pay, your pay schedule and your contract all come from the startup.</p>
<p>Employees are usually paid through payroll, with tax taken off where the law requires it. Contractors usually send an invoice and are paid by bank transfer or a payment service such as Wise or Payoneer, and they handle their own tax. Ask in the interview how often you will be paid, in which currency, and by which method, and agree it in writing. Note that PayPal does not operate in some countries, so confirm a method that works where you live.</p>
<p>There is no escrow, so there is no payment protection. If a startup pays late or runs out of money, Wellfound cannot release your pay, because it never held it. Startups can fail, so keep an emergency fund and do not rely on one early stage employer for all of your income if you can avoid it.</p>
<h2 id="what-it-costs-and-what-to-expect">What it costs and what to expect</h2>
<p>Browsing and applying on Wellfound cost you nothing, and there are no subscription fees for job seekers. Your real cost is time. A focused search takes about thirty minutes a few times a week, and a good profile takes about an hour to build well.</p>
<p>Example only: suppose you find a remote contract marketing role at an assumed rate of 30 US dollars per hour, and you work an assumed 20 hours per week. One week pays 20 times 30, which is 600 US dollars, and four weeks pay 2,400 US dollars before tax. These figures are assumed for the example. Real pay rates vary by role, startup, stage and country, and many applicants never get hired at all.</p>
<p>Expect a slow start. Response rates depend on role fit, seniority and profile quality, and some users report hearing little back. A realistic first month is week one for your profile and preferences, weeks two and three for ten to twenty well matched applications, and week four for interviews and follow ups. If you hear back from none, improve your profile results and links before you apply to more roles.</p>
<p>Results vary with your experience, your location, demand for your skills, the time you spend, competition, your expenses and platform fees. Tax rules differ by country, so check your local tax authority or a qualified accountant.</p>
<h2 id="common-mistakes">Common mistakes</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sending an application before checking your note and CV, when an application cannot be withdrawn once sent.</li>
<li>Leaving your profile status hidden, so recruiters cannot find or contact you.</li>
<li>Applying to roles without reading the salary, equity, location and stage details first.</li>
<li>Waiting on one application forever, instead of checking the applied list and moving on after expiry.</li>
<li>Listing job titles with no results, links or proof of work in your profile.</li>
<li>Accepting low cash pay for equity without understanding vesting or the risk of failure.</li>
<li>Starting work before pay, equity and the payment method are agreed in writing.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="risks-and-scams-to-watch-for">Risks and scams to watch for</h2>
<p>Wellfound is an established platform, but startups vary in quality, and scammers misuse job board names. Learn the warning signs before you apply.</p>
<p>First, never pay to get hired. A real startup will not ask you for an upfront fee, training costs or equipment money before you start. The US Federal Trade Commission gives the same warning in its <a href="https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams" rel="noopener" target="_blank">job scams advice</a>.</p>
<p>Second, beware of spam and vague outreach. Some users report spam messages instead of real employers. A real founder interviews you properly and sends a written contract. An offer after one short chat, with high pay for simple work, is a warning sign.</p>
<p>Third, protect your personal details. Do not share bank details or copies of identity documents until you have a written contract with a company you have verified. Never cash a cheque from an employer and send part of the money back, because that is a fake cheque scam.</p>
<p>Fourth, check the company yourself. Search the startup name plus the words scam, review or complaint. Check the company website, its team, its funding stage and its product. Early stage startups carry real risk, including late pay and sudden closure, so ask direct questions about runway and pay practices. Our guide on <a href="https://earnquill.com/online-jobs/how-to-spot-online-job-scams" rel="noopener" target="_blank">how to spot online job scams</a> lists the full set of checks.</p>
<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 id="is-wellfound-free-for-job-seekers">Is Wellfound free for job seekers?</h3>
<p>Yes. Browsing, creating a profile and applying on Wellfound are free for job seekers as of November 2026. Companies can post jobs for free and pay for optional extras, and that is how the platform makes its money.</p>
<h3 id="is-wellfound-the-same-as-angellist-talent">Is Wellfound the same as AngelList Talent?</h3>
<p>Wellfound is the new name for AngelList Talent. The talent marketplace spun out as an independent company and rebranded as Wellfound in November 2022. AngelList continues separately, focused on venture and fund work.</p>
<h3 id="does-wellfound-pay-you-directly">Does Wellfound pay you directly?</h3>
<p>No. Wellfound never pays job seekers. The startup who hires you pays your salary or contract fee directly, usually through payroll or by bank transfer. Wellfound takes no fee from your pay and holds no money for you.</p>
<h3 id="is-wellfound-legit">Is Wellfound legit?</h3>
<p>Yes. Wellfound is an established hiring platform used by thousands of startups. That does not mean every startup is honest or stable, so verify each company yourself before you share personal details or start work.</p>
<h3 id="can-beginners-find-work-on-wellfound">Can beginners find work on Wellfound?</h3>
<p>Yes, but it is harder. Most listings target experienced talent, so beginners do better with early stage startups that value generalists. A complete profile with results and links gets more replies than a thin one.</p>
<h3 id="how-does-equity-work-in-wellfound-jobs">How does equity work in Wellfound jobs?</h3>
<p>Listings show equity alongside salary, usually as stock options. Equity vests over time and may be worth a lot or nothing, depending on the startup. Ask about the amount, the vesting schedule and what happens if you leave, and get it in writing.</p>
<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2>
<p>Wellfound is worth using if you want to work at a startup and you value seeing salary and equity before you apply. It is free, applications go directly to founders, and contract roles can last months. It cannot find you a job, guarantee a reply, or protect your pay, and startups carry real risk, so your profile, your checks and your written agreement do the real work.</p>
