How to Earn Money on SolidGigs in 2026

Learning how to earn money on SolidGigs starts with correcting one common mistake. SolidGigs is not a marketplace where clients hire you. You cannot earn money directly on SolidGigs, because no hiring or payment happens there. SolidGigs is a paid lead service. You pay a monthly subscription, and SolidGigs sends you curated freelance job leads, helps you pitch for them, and, on higher plans, builds you a website and handles outreach. You earn when you win a client from those leads and do the work. This guide explains how that works in 2026, what it costs, and whether it is worth it.

What SolidGigs is, and how to earn money on SolidGigs

SolidGigs is a subscription service for freelancers who want steady client work. It finds freelance job leads from across the web, filters them for quality, and delivers the ones that match your skills to your inbox every weekday. You then pitch for the leads you want, agree terms directly with the client, and keep 100% of what the client pays.

That distinction matters. On Upwork or Fiverr, the platform sits between you and the client, holds the payment, and takes a fee. On SolidGigs, there is no platform between you and the client once you have the lead. You work directly with the client and agree your own price.

SolidGigs is also not a job board. A job board lists every job it can find, and you must search through them yourself. SolidGigs curates its leads, which means it reviews jobs and sends you only the ones that match the skills and client type you chose when you signed up. That saves time, but you still have to pitch for every lead. No lead is a guaranteed job.

How SolidGigs works in five steps

The process follows the same pattern every week.

  1. Sign up and set your filters. You answer questions about your skills, your niche, and the kind of clients you want.
  2. Receive leads every weekday, matched to your filters.
  3. Review the leads and choose the ones worth pitching.
  4. Pitch fast with a tailored message, using the AI generated pitches as a starting point.
  5. Win the client and work directly with them. You agree the price, do the work, and keep 100% of the payment.

On higher plans, SolidGigs also builds you a portfolio website and handles some marketing and outreach. That work runs in the background while you pitch, but it does not replace pitching. The leads still need a fast, personal response from you.

The three plans and current prices

SolidGigs lists three plans on its homepage, all billed annually, all with a 14 day free trial and 0 US dollars due today. The prices below were checked on the official homepage on 2026-11-19. Older reviews cite very different figures, such as about 19 US dollars per month and a 2 US dollar trial, and those figures are out of date. Check the homepage again before you pay.

Pro 99 US dollars (was 149) Unlimited opportunities and pitches, done for you portfolio website with custom domain and professional email, 3 website updates a month, 1 new case study a month

The Pipeline plan suits a freelancer who only needs leads and pitches. The Pro plan suits a freelancer who also wants a professional website to send clients to, because a good website makes you look credible and helps you win work. The Expert plan suits a freelancer who wants SolidGigs to handle most of their marketing and can afford a high monthly cost. For most freelancers, the choice is between Pipeline and Pro.

The 90 day guarantee, explained honestly

SolidGigs offers a 90 day new client guarantee on all plans, according to the pricing section of its homepage. The wording is clear. If you follow the SolidGigs system and do not get a new client within 90 days, SolidGigs will keep working for free until you do. That sounds reassuring, but read it with care.

First, the guarantee promises more work from SolidGigs, not a refund and not a client. If you do not win a client in 90 days, you get extra months of the service at no cost, not your money back. Second, the guarantee depends on following the SolidGigs system, which means using the leads, sending pitches, and doing the work the service asks of you. If you sign up and never pitch, the guarantee is unlikely to apply. Third, one answer on the site's own FAQ page describes the guarantee in terms of the Pro and Expert plans, while the pricing section states that it applies to all three. If the guarantee matters to your decision, confirm which plans it covers with SolidGigs before you pay.

The homepage also shows claims such as winning a first gig in 8 days for 2,230 US dollars, and similar figures for other members. Treat those as marketing examples, not as typical results. The site's own footer states that statistics are estimated based on user input and that results are not typical.

What you actually get: leads, AI pitches, website and outreach

The core of SolidGigs is the lead feed. Leads arrive every weekday, filtered to your skills and client type. The Pipeline plan gives up to 100 opportunities a month, and higher plans give unlimited opportunities. The number that matters is how many leads fit you well enough to pitch. A lead for a skill you do not offer is not useful, however many arrive.

The AI pitches are a starting point, not a finished message. SolidGigs generates a draft pitch for each lead, and the Pipeline plan includes 10 a month while the higher plans include unlimited pitches. The site itself advises you to personalise the pitch before you send it, and that advice is right. A generic pitch will lose to a short message that mentions the client's project by name and explains why you fit it. Use the AI draft to save time, then rewrite the opening and the closing in your own words.

The website on the Pro and Expert plans is a done for you portfolio site, built from a set of designs, with your own custom domain and a professional email address. Your website goes live in less than 14 days, according to the site. The Pro plan includes 3 website updates a month and 1 new case study a month, while Expert includes unlimited updates and case studies. After 12 months, you own your website outright, according to the site's FAQ page.

The outreach on the Expert plan adds 30 personalised outreaches a month, 20 social media posts a month, and 5 short form videos a month, plus a dedicated account manager. That is a lot of marketing help, but at 839 US dollars a month billed annually, it only makes sense if your freelance income is already high enough.

How to earn money on SolidGigs and make the subscription pay

A subscription only pays if the work it brings is worth more than its cost. Start with the 14 day free trial, and use it properly. Set your filters with care on day one, and pitch for the best leads at once. Do not spend the trial browsing. The trial is your chance to test whether the leads fit your skills, and you can only learn that by pitching.

Track your numbers from the first week. Note how many leads arrive, how many you pitch for, and how many turn into paid work. Then do the maths. On the Pipeline plan at 29 US dollars a month, one small project a month usually covers the cost many times over. On the Pro plan at 99 US dollars a month, you need more work to cover it. On the Expert plan at 839 US dollars a month, you need a large and steady income.

Speed matters more than volume. Opportunities move fast, and members who respond on the same day land work faster than those who wait. Check your leads each morning, and send a tailored pitch the same day. Follow up once if you hear nothing, then move on. Keep your portfolio ready, so a client who replies can see your work at once.

Who should join and who should not

SolidGigs suits freelancers who already have a marketable skill and a portfolio, and who lose too much time hunting for work. Writers, designers, developers, marketers, virtual assistants, consultants, and data specialists all fit, because SolidGigs lists leads across many niches. It suits freelancers who can pitch, respond fast, and want direct clients.

It does not suit beginners with no portfolio, because leads still need a pitch and proof of skill. The site says you do not need years of experience, only a skill you can already sell, but a beginner with nothing to show will still struggle to win pitches. It does not suit freelancers with no budget, because the subscription costs money every month whether or not you win work. It does not suit people who want a guaranteed income, because SolidGigs does not guarantee work or income. If you are new, build a portfolio first with our guide to creating a freelance portfolio.

What it costs and what to expect

Learning how to earn money on SolidGigs costs 29, 99, or 839 US dollars per month, billed annually, depending on the plan, with a 14 day free trial and 0 US dollars due today. Billed annually means you pay for a year at once: Pipeline costs 348 US dollars a year, Pro costs 1,188 US dollars a year, and Expert costs 10,068 US dollars a year. Make sure you can afford the yearly total, not just the monthly figure.

SolidGigs saves you search time, but it does not find you clients. You still have to pitch, follow up, and win the work. Some members may win a first client in their first week, but the site's own footer says results are not typical. A realistic expectation is that you will see relevant leads within days, pitch for several in your first two weeks, and win work only if your pitch is strong.

Worked example, example only: these numbers are not typical results. Imagine you join the Pipeline plan at 29 US dollars a month billed annually, which is 348 US dollars a year. In your first month you pitch for 12 leads, win 1 project worth 600 US dollars, and keep all of it because SolidGigs takes no commission. Your subscription cost is 29 US dollars, so the project covers it more than twenty times over. Now imagine a month where you pitch for 12 leads and win none. Your cost is still 29 US dollars, and your income from SolidGigs is zero. The service is only worth it if your win rate makes the projects worth more than the subscription.

Common mistakes

  • Sending AI generated pitches without personalising them, which clients ignore.
  • Relying only on SolidGigs and letting your other client channels go quiet.

Risks and scams to watch for

The main risk in learning how to earn money on SolidGigs is paying for months without winning enough work to cover the cost. Control it by tracking your leads, pitches, and wins, and cancel if the numbers do not work. The site states that you can switch plans or cancel before the trial ends, and that after the trial you move to the plan you selected. Check the cancellation terms in your account before the trial ends, because this guide could not confirm whether the trial needs a card or the full refund terms beyond the guarantee wording.

Watch for scams that target freelancers looking for leads. SolidGigs itself is a real company, based in Kaysville, Utah, and contactable at team@solidgigs.com, but scammers may copy its name. Be wary of anyone who asks you to pay a fee outside the official site, asks for your bank details by message, or promises guaranteed work for a fee. Real leads come through your SolidGigs account, and real clients discuss the project before they discuss payment. In the United States, you can report fraud to the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Frequently asked questions

Is SolidGigs legit?

SolidGigs is a real subscription service that has operated since 2017, and its homepage states that over 30,800 freelancers have used it. It is a lead service, not a marketplace, so it does not hire you or pay you directly. Whether it is worth it depends on whether its leads turn into paid work for you.

How much does SolidGigs cost?

SolidGigs costs 29, 99, or 839 US dollars per month billed annually, as of November 2026, with a 14 day free trial and 0 US dollars due today. Older reviews that cite about 19 US dollars a month are out of date.

Does SolidGigs take a commission?

No, SolidGigs does not take a commission. You keep 100% of what your clients pay you, and 100% of the client relationship, because SolidGigs never handles your client payments. You pay only the monthly subscription.

Does SolidGigs guarantee work?

No, SolidGigs does not guarantee work or income. It offers a 90 day new client guarantee, which means that if you follow its system and do not get a new client within 90 days, it will keep working for free until you do. That is extra service, not a refund and not a promised client.

Is SolidGigs worth it for beginners?

SolidGigs can help a beginner who already has a skill to sell and a small portfolio, because it saves search time and provides pitch drafts. It is less useful for a beginner with no portfolio and no work to show, because leads still need a strong pitch. Beginners should build a portfolio first, then test the 14 day trial.

How does SolidGigs work?

To learn how to earn money on SolidGigs, sign up, set your skills and client filters, and receive curated job leads every weekday. You review the leads, pitch for the ones that fit, and agree terms directly with the client. Higher plans add a done for you portfolio website and outreach help. You keep all of your client payments.

Conclusion

Learning how to earn money on SolidGigs comes down to a test. Use the 14 day trial to see if the leads fit your skills, pitch fast, and track whether the work you win is worth more than the subscription. The Pipeline plan at 29 US dollars a month is a low cost test, the Pro plan adds a website, and the Expert plan is only for freelancers with a high income already. Remember that SolidGigs finds leads, not clients, and that the 90 day guarantee promises more service, not a refund.
