How to Earn Money with YouTube Super Chat and Super Thanks in 2026

Learning how to earn money with YouTube Super Chat and Super Thanks is worth doing if you already go live or publish videos that viewers love. These two features, called Supers, let viewers pay to support you directly. Super Chat works during a live stream. Super Thanks works on a video after it is published. This guide shows you how each one works, who can switch them on, how much you keep, and how you get paid.

This guide is for beginners who already have a YouTube channel. For the general setup steps, read how to start a YouTube channel.

Super Chat versus Super Thanks: the side by side comparison

Super Chat is for live streams and Premieres. A viewer buys a highlighted message in the live chat. The message stands out in colour, and a larger payment can pin it to the top for a time. Super Thanks is for long-form videos and Shorts. A viewer buys a one time animation that only the buyer sees, plus a distinct, colourful, and customisable comment. The comment shows the amount paid. Super Thanks is not a crowdfunding tool, and it is not a donation tool. YouTube Help says so plainly on its Buy Super Thanks page.

Feature Super Chat Super Thanks
When it earns During a live stream or a Premiere After a video or Short is published
Where the viewer pays In the live chat Below a long-form video or on a Short
What the viewer gets A highlighted chat message, sometimes pinned A one time animation plus a colourful comment
How long it lasts Pinned for a time, then it scrolls away The comment stays unless it is removed
Best for Live Q and A, reactions, community streams Evergreen tutorials and reviews

Super Chat rewards a creator who goes live often. Super Thanks rewards a creator whose videos keep earning views for months.

Who can switch Supers on

Supers are part of the YouTube Partner Program, also called YPP. YPP is the programme that lets creators earn money from YouTube. To join at the full tier, a channel needs 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. That is stated on the YouTube Partner Program overview page checked on 2026-11-11. You also need to follow the channel monetisation policies, live in a country where the programme is available, have no active Community Guidelines strikes, have 2-Step Verification turned on, have advanced features access, and have one active AdSense for YouTube account linked. AdSense for YouTube is the Google payment system that pays creators. Meeting the threshold does not guarantee acceptance, and the review typically takes about 1 month. For the full picture, read how to monetise a YouTube channel.

A lower fan funding tier is also reported. Guides citing the YouTube Help expanded programme page report that a channel with 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days can unlock memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, Shopping, and Creator Partnerships, but no ad revenue. Treat this tier as reported, because the overview page as opened on 2026-11-11 lists only the full tier.

From February 1, 2027, the rules for new applicants are reported to change. YouTube announced on 10 August 2026, according to several independent guides, that new applicants for the ad revenue tier will need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. The fan funding tier is reported to stay unchanged. Confirm the current terms in YouTube Studio.

Country rules matter as much as subscriber counts. A guide that checked the YouTube Help country lists on 1 October 2026 states that on that date the Super Chat and Super Thanks lists named the same countries, including Australia, Canada, India, Ireland, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, and the United States, while Pakistan and Ukraine were on neither list. The buying list on the Buy Super Thanks page, as opened on 2026-11-11, also does not include Pakistan. Lists change, so check in YouTube Studio under Earn, then Supers.

Step 1: Check your eligibility and your country

Open YouTube Studio on a computer and click Earn in the left menu. If you see a Supers tab, your channel is eligible. If you do not see it, check your subscriber count, watch hours, and Shorts views. A channel set as made for kids cannot use Supers, and an active Community Guidelines strike also blocks YPP access. Turn on 2-Step Verification, and check that you have advanced features access.

Step 2: Accept the Commerce Product Module and switch Supers on

The Commerce Product Module, also called CPM, is the agreement you must accept before you can earn from Super Thanks. YouTube Help states on its Super Thanks manage page that to earn from Super Thanks, you and any network that manages your channel must first accept the Commerce Product Module. On a computer, sign in to YouTube Studio, click Earn, then click the Supers tab. Follow the on-screen instructions to sign the Commerce Product Module. Once you finish, Super Thanks appears next to a switch. It then appears on all eligible previous and future Shorts and long-form videos. There is no per video setting.

On a phone, the flow is different. In the YouTube Studio mobile app, tap Earn, then tap the Supers card. The mobile flow switches on every Supers product at once. On a computer, you can switch each Supers product on or off on its own. To switch Super Thanks off later, use the Supers tab on a computer.

Step 3: Plan a live stream that earns Super Chats

Super Chat only earns while you are live, so plan the stream around conversation. Pick a topic your viewers already ask about, such as a Q and A or a live test of a product. Tell viewers in advance when you will go live. During the stream, read Super Chat messages out loud and answer them first. Viewers pay to be noticed, so a creator who ignores paid messages will not get many more. Thank each supporter by name, and keep the thanks short. Read how to make money with YouTube live for the wider live streaming plan.

Do not beg for Super Chats, and do not promise a reward in exchange for one. Do not run a prize, a raffle, or a contest that picks a paid supporter at random. Do not ask viewers under 18 to send money. YouTube Help says Supers are not a crowdfunding or donation tool.

Moderation matters during a live stream. YouTube Help says you can moderate Super Chat messages, and paid messages must follow the Community Guidelines. Set up moderators you trust before you go live. If a paid message breaks the rules, remove it. A removed paid message is not refunded.

Step 4: Earn Super Thanks from your videos and Shorts

Super Thanks earns quietly, in the background, from videos you already published. Once you switch it on, it appears on all eligible previous and future long-form videos and Shorts. The best videos for Super Thanks are the ones viewers return to: a tutorial that solved a real problem, or a review that helped someone choose. You cannot switch Super Thanks on for one video only, and you cannot set a different price for one video.

At the end of a video, you can mention Super Thanks once, in one sentence. For example: "If this video saved you time, the Super Thanks button below the video is one way to support the channel." Do not spend a minute on it. Reply to Super Thanks comments when you see them. YouTube Studio lets you filter comments to show only those from Super Thanks. YouTube Help also lets you reply to a Super Thanks comment with a Short, using the Comment Sticker feature.

Some videos cannot earn Super Thanks, even when the feature is switched on. Guides that checked the YouTube Help eligibility page list these exclusions: a video set as made for kids, a video with comments turned off, a video with a copyright claim, a video linked to a YouTube Giving fundraiser, and channels under a music rights contract that blocks fan funding. If Super Thanks does not appear on one video but appears on others, check those five points first. Super Chat may not be available with supervised accounts, which are accounts with extra controls for younger viewers.

Step 5: Use the archived video workflow to earn twice from one stream

This is the workflow most competitor guides miss. Super Thanks is not available on a live stream or a Premiere while it is live. It becomes available on the archived long-form version afterwards. That rule is stated on the YouTube Help Super Thanks eligibility page as checked by a guide on 1 October 2026, and the manage page supports it by saying Super Thanks appears on all eligible previous and future videos.

First, go live and earn Super Chats during the stream. Second, let the stream archive as a long-form video. Do not delete it, and do not set it to private, because a private video cannot earn Super Thanks. Third, check the archived video. Make sure comments are turned on, and make sure the video is not set as made for kids and has no copyright claim. Fourth, give the archived video a clear title and description. Read how to make YouTube videos that get views for title and description basics.

A Q and A stream works best for this workflow, because the archived video still answers real questions months later. A reaction stream works less well, because the news it reacts to goes out of date fast. If the archive is age restricted, unlisted, or private, Super Thanks may not appear, so keep the archive public and clean.

How much viewers pay: reported prices and pin times

YouTube does not publish a single official price list for Supers in the help pages opened for this guide, so treat every price below as reported by guides, not as an official figure. Guides report that a Super Chat costs from 1 to 500 US dollars. The amount a viewer pays decides how long the message stays pinned. One competitor guide lists these pin times, as reported:

Reported Super Chat amount Reported pin time
1 to 4.99 US dollars 30 seconds
5 to 9.99 US dollars 1 minute
10 to 24.99 US dollars 2 minutes
25 to 49.99 US dollars 5 minutes
50 to 99.99 US dollars 10 minutes
100 US dollars or more Up to 1 hour

Guides also report that Super Thanks price points run from 2 to 50 US dollars, commonly listed as 2, 5, 10, 20, and 50 US dollars. Guides disagree on the count, with one guide saying four fixed points and another listing five, and neither cites an official price list on the day. Prices also vary by country. Never promise a pin time, because YouTube can change the tiers.

Viewer spending limits also vary by country. YouTube Help states on its Buy Super Thanks page that in general a viewer can spend up to 500 US dollars per day, or 2,000 US dollars per week, on Super Chats, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, and Gifts combined. That is a limit per viewer, not a limit per creator.

How you get paid: the 70 percent share, tax, and the payout threshold

You keep 70 percent of Supers revenue, and YouTube keeps 30 percent. For Super Thanks, YouTube Help states this directly on the manage page: creators get 70 percent of Super Thanks revenue recognised by Google, calculated after taxes and fees are deducted, including App Store fees on iOS. Transaction costs, including credit card fees, are currently covered by YouTube. For Super Chat, YouTube Help states the same 70 percent share, in the search index version of its Super Chat manage page, because the Revenue Share section did not render in a direct open on 2026-11-11. So 70 percent is a share after taxes and fees, not 70 percent of the full amount the viewer paid.

You get Supers revenue in the same way as ad revenue, through AdSense for YouTube. Supers earnings are combined with your other YouTube earnings in the same AdSense account. The AdSense for YouTube payment threshold is 100 US dollars. If your total YouTube earnings in a month are below 100 US dollars, you are not paid that month, and the balance carries over. Guides that track YouTube Help report that finalised earnings reach AdSense for YouTube between the 7th and 12th of the following month. Finalised earnings are the earnings YouTube has checked and confirmed for that month. In YouTube Studio, you can see your Supers revenue under Revenue, then the How you make money card, then Supers. Guides also report that the Supers tab shows Super Chat and Super Sticker purchases for the past 30 days only, so check your numbers monthly.

Supers income is taxable income, not a tax free gift. Keep a record of every payment, including the date, the amount, and the currency, and set aside money for tax. Rules differ by country. Check your local tax authority or a qualified accountant about how to declare YouTube income where you live. Payments are non-refundable. YouTube Help states on its Buy Super Thanks page that Super Thanks voluntary payments are non-refundable, including when the comment is deleted, moderated, or removed. If a Super Thanks comment is removed for violating policies, YouTube will donate its own portion of revenue to charity. Super Chat payments follow the same non-refundable rule, according to guides that checked the policy pages.

When a viewer buys Super Thanks, their comment, channel name, profile picture, and purchase amount are visible to the public. YouTube Help also says that information may be available through the YouTube Data API and shared with third party services.

Worked example: what a small channel could earn from Supers

Example only: these numbers are not typical results. They show how the maths works, so you can test your own figures. No guide publishes a typical Super Chat or Super Thanks amount, and YouTube publishes no average.

Imagine a small channel that goes live 4 times a month and publishes 4 videos a month. In one month, it receives 25 Super Chats at an average of 5 US dollars each. That is 25 times 5, which is 125 US dollars in viewer payments. At a 70 percent creator share, that is 125 times 70 percent, which is 87.50 US dollars. In the same month, it receives 20 Super Thanks payments at an average of 5 US dollars each. That is 20 times 5, which is 100 US dollars in viewer payments. At a 70 percent creator share, that is 100 times 70 percent, which is 70 US dollars. Add the two shares together, and the channel earns 87.50 plus 70, which is 157.50 US dollars from Supers in that month, before tax.

Recheck: 25 times 5 is 125. 125 times 70 percent is 87.50. 20 times 5 is 100. 100 times 70 percent is 70. 87.50 plus 70 is 157.50. The maths checks out as an illustration, not a promise.

Now look at the payout threshold. The AdSense for YouTube payment threshold is 100 US dollars. In this example, the channel passes it, with 157.50 US dollars, so it would be paid for that month. But if the same channel received only the 20 Super Thanks payments and no Super Chats, its share would be 70 US dollars, which is below the threshold, so it would not be paid that month, and the 70 US dollars would carry over. One competitor guide gives example monthly earnings tables with figures up to 3,000 US dollars and more, but those figures are invented scenarios with no source, so this guide does not repeat them.

How to earn money with YouTube Super Chat and Super Thanks

Go live on a regular schedule, so viewers know when to find you. A weekly live stream at the same time builds a habit, and regular viewers are the ones who pay. Make videos that solve a problem, because Super Thanks follows gratitude. Thank supporters well. Read the message, answer the question, and move on. Build the archive too, so past streams keep earning Super Thanks.

Combine Supers with other income, so a slow month does not hurt you. Read how to use YouTube for affiliate marketing and how to get YouTube sponsorships to add income routes that do not depend on live viewers. Grow the audience that pays. Read how to grow a YouTube channel for the long term plan. A channel with 500 loyal viewers will earn more from Supers than a channel with 5,000 viewers who never comment.

What it costs and what to expect

Switching Supers on costs nothing. There is no fee to join YPP, no fee to accept the Commerce Product Module, and no fee to switch on Super Chat or Super Thanks. Expect 2 to 4 hours per live stream when you include planning, streaming, and the archive tidy up, and 5 to 10 hours per long-form video when you include research, scripting, filming, and editing.

Expect a slow start. Supers pay nothing until your channel is eligible, and then they pay small amounts at first. Most viewers never pay, and the few who do usually pay small amounts. Your first Supers payment may take months after you switch the features on, and some months may bring nothing at all. Results vary with your niche, your viewers' country, how often you go live, and how loyal your audience is. Nothing here is a promise of income.

Common mistakes

  • Switching Supers on and then never going live, so Super Chat has no chance to earn.
  • Begging for Super Chats, or promising a reward in exchange for a payment.
  • Treating Supers as a fundraiser or a donation drive, when YouTube Help says they are not.
  • Ignoring paid messages during a live stream, so supporters stop paying.
  • Deleting a live stream archive, or setting it to private, so Super Thanks cannot appear on it.
  • Turning comments off on a video, which also blocks Super Thanks on that video.
  • Stating a price, a pin time, or a payout figure as official, when guides only report it.
  • Expecting a payout in a month when total YouTube earnings are below the 100 US dollar AdSense threshold.
  • Spending Super Chat income before it is finalised, or before tax is set aside.
  • Asking viewers under 18 to send money, or running a prize draw for paid supporters.

Risks and scams to watch for

Watch out for viewers who send a large Super Chat and then ask for something in return, such as a shoutout that breaks YouTube rules, a link to their own channel, or personal contact details. A payment does not buy rule breaking, and you can remove a paid message that breaks the Community Guidelines. Watch out for fake support messages. A scammer may pose as a supporter in chat, send a small payment, and then ask you or your viewers to click a link, join another platform, or send money back. Do not click links from strangers in live chat.

Watch out for chargeback scams. A viewer may pay, then claim the payment was fraud. Because Supers payments are non-refundable, you should not offer to refund a payment yourself. Watch out for anyone who offers to boost your Super Chat income for a fee, or sells you fake viewers for a live stream. Those offers are scams, and fake engagement can cost you monetisation.
